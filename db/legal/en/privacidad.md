> **In short.** We keep your data to train you and track your progress. We use your health data only with your express consent, and only your coach and the person who runs the gym see it. We don't sell your data or use it for third-party advertising. Your photos are published only if you authorize it separately, and you can withdraw that permission at any time. You can get a copy of your data, correct it or delete it for free, on WhatsApp, at the gym or from *My data* in the portal.

This is a translation. The Spanish version is the one that applies under Argentine law.

**What's required.** To train you we need your identity and contact details, the fitness declaration and your evaluation data: without them we can't provide the service. Your fingerprint, publishing your photos and offers are optional: if you don't give them, you still train.

**We never see or store your card details.** When you pay with Mercado Pago, Mercado Pago collects and protects them.

## 3. What we use it for

- **To provide the service you bought:** evaluate you, build your plan, track your progress, book your sessions, remind you of your next evaluation and tell you about the service. This is what the contract requires.
- **Your health data,** to train you safely and measure your progress, only with your **express consent** (art. 7 of Law 25,326). The fitness declaration also because Buenos Aires City law requires it to train at a gym (Law 139, as amended by Law 6316).
- **Your fingerprint,** only if you agree, so you can enter the gym without keys or cards.
- **Your photos on the website and social media,** only if you authorize it separately (art. 53 of the Argentine Civil and Commercial Code). Without that authorization, your progress photos are private.
- **To charge and invoice,** and meet our tax and legal obligations.
- **To send you news and offers by email.** Every email has a link to unsubscribe, and you can ask at any time (art. 27 of Law 25,326).
- **To understand how the website is used** and improve it, with Google Analytics statistics.
- **To comply with the law** and defend our rights or yours in case of a claim.

We don't sell or rent your data. We don't use it to make automated decisions that affect you.

## 4. Your health data and your fingerprint

Health data and fingerprints are **sensitive data**: we protect them more than anything else.

- **Nobody is required to give us sensitive data** (art. 7 of Law 25,326). But without your health information we can't evaluate you or plan your training safely, and without the fitness declaration Buenos Aires City law doesn't allow us to train you.
- **Who sees it:** your assigned coach and the person who runs the gym. The front desk doesn't see your health data, and the website chat has no access to your health data or your ID number.
- **The orthopedic doctor** in the Premium System sees you as a health professional. The doctor keeps your medical record, under professional secrecy and Argentine Law 26,529 on Patients' Rights, and only shares with the gym what is needed to train you (for example, which exercises to avoid), and with your agreement.
- **Your fingerprint** is stored only in the door reader, to recognize you when you come in. We don't use it for anything else. If you don't want to use it, we give you another way in. We delete it from the reader when you stop being a client.
- **You can withdraw these permissions at any time,** from *My data* in the portal or by asking us. Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before with your permission.

## 6. Cookies and statistics

Cookies are small files the website stores in your browser.

- **Necessary:** Clerk's, to keep you signed in to the portal; Cloudflare's, to protect the website from attacks; the portal language cookie; and the chat cookie, so it knows who you are if you're signed in. The website doesn't work properly without them.
- **Statistics:** Google Analytics cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_…`), to count visits and see which pages are used. Google Analytics doesn't store your IP address; it only infers an approximate location.
- **We don't use advertising cookies** or remarketing.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser, and turn off Google Analytics with [Google's add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Your client file, evaluations, photos and plan:** while you're a client and up to 2 years after your last check-in or payment, so you can come back with your history. Then we delete them. If you ask us to delete them sooner, we do.
- **Your fingerprint:** we delete it from the reader within 30 days after you stop being a client.
- **Payments and invoices:** as long as tax and commercial laws require (up to 10 years).
- **Your choices and your cancellation requests:** up to 5 years, as a record for you and for us.
- **Google Analytics statistics:** up to 14 months.
- **Photos published with your authorization:** until you withdraw it.

## 8. Your rights

You can, **free of charge**:

- **Get a copy of your data** (right of access). We answer within **10 calendar days**. You can ask for free every 6 months, or sooner if you have a legitimate interest (art. 14 of Law 25,326).
- **Correct it, update it or have it deleted.** We do it within **5 business days** (art. 16). We only keep what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices.
- **Withdraw your permissions** (health, fingerprint, photos and offers) at any time.
- **Ask us to stop sending you advertising,** or to remove you fully or partly from our offers database (art. 27).

To do so, go to *My data* in the portal, message us on WhatsApp at {whatsapp} or {correo}, or ask at the gym. To protect your data, we may ask you to confirm it's you.

If we don't answer or you disagree with our answer, you can file a complaint with the [Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales), Argentina's data protection authority, or bring a habeas data action.

## 9. How we protect your data

- The website and portal only work over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
- Each team member sees only what they need for their job: your coach, your data; the front desk, payments and accounts, not your health.
- Photos and certificates are not public: the portal shows them only to people allowed to see them.
- Access keys to our services are not in the website code.

No system is infallible. If an incident affected your data, we would tell you and the AAIP, and explain what we did.

## 10. Minors

People under 18 train with us only with the authorization of their mother, father or guardian, who gives the permissions in this policy on their behalf and can exercise their rights. We don't publish photos of minors.

## 11. If you train from another country

If you buy an online plan from outside Argentina, your data is processed in Argentina and with the providers in section 5. The European Union recognizes Argentina as a country with an adequate level of data protection.

If you live in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Brazil, we process your data to perform the contract, your health data with your explicit consent, and website statistics and security on the basis of our legitimate interest. In addition to the rights in section 8, you can ask us to restrict the use of your data, object to it, receive your data in a portable format, and complain to the data protection authority in your country.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change something important, we'll tell you by email or in the portal before it applies, and the portal will ask you to accept again. The date of the latest version is at the top of this page.

## 13. Legal notice

The data subject has the right to access their personal data free of charge at intervals of no less than six months, unless a legitimate interest is shown, under article 14(3) of Law No. 25,326. The AGENCIA DE ACCESO A LA INFORMACIÓN PÚBLICA, as the supervisory authority of Law No. 25,326, has the power to handle complaints and claims from anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with personal data protection rules.
