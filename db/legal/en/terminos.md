## 5. Your health and safety

**Before you start.** You fill in the **sworn fitness declaration** that Buenos Aires City requires to train at a gym (Law 139, as amended by Law 6316), and update it whenever your health changes and at least once a year. If the declaration calls for it, or your coach asks, you need a **medical certificate** saying you can train. If you train online, we ask for the same declaration.

**What we ask of you.** Tell us the truth about your health: injuries, conditions, medication, pregnancy and any change. Follow your coach's instructions, use the equipment as we show you, and stop and tell us if you feel pain, dizziness or anything unusual.

**What we do.** Qualified staff train you, we adapt the training to your health and goal, we keep the equipment and the gym in good condition, and in an emergency we give first aid and call emergency services.

**Liability.** We are liable for damage caused by our service, as the law provides. Nothing in these terms limits that liability or your consumer rights.

**Results.** Every body responds differently: we don't promise a specific result. The transformations we show are from real clients who authorized it, not a guarantee.

**Nutrition and supplements.** The nutrition and supplement guidance your coach gives you is general, to support your training: it is not a medical diet or treatment. If you have an illness or need a nutrition plan, see a doctor or a licensed nutritionist: we recommend adding one. If you have allergies, a medical condition or are pregnant, tell us before taking any drink or supplement at the gym.

**The orthopedic doctor** in the Premium System sees you as a health professional, under their own professional responsibility. They don't replace your regular doctor.
