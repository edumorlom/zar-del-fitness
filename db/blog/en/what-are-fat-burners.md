Basically, fat burners are pills with a blend of thermogenics that helps speed up the metabolism, because it contains natural ingredients such as ephedra, HCA, chitosan and pyruvate that stimulate energy.

It’s important to point out that the benefits listed below were gathered from analyses of and interviews with people who have used these supplements, but there are no conclusive medical studies that fully recommend these fat-burning pills.

- The most popular burner is the one that contains caffeine. Caffeine stimulates the central nervous system, supporting mental work and the release of adrenaline.
- They mobilize fatty acids by facilitating the release of calcium, which in turn helps burn muscle fat.
- Burners with L-carnitine oxidize fats so they can be used throughout the day and burned.
- They increase the metabolic rate by up to 7%, which translates into weight loss.
- Caffeine-based burners improve athletic performance for swimmers, runners and cyclists.
- Green tea–based fat burners contain polyphenols, specifically catechins, which are responsible for reducing fat accumulation. Green tea also increases calorie burning by 3.5%.
- There are burners with an active substance called synephrine, which stimulates the metabolism and speeds up fat burning.

These also contain a range of other, non-thermogenic ingredients, such as hydroxycitric acid, which suppresses fat synthesis, and chromium picolinate, a mineral that maintains proper glucose levels and regulates hunger.

What is true is that fat burners can contain a variety of natural and chemical ingredients that may not work the same way for everyone and aren’t always clearly specified on their labels. That’s why it’s important to know that if you have any of these conditions, you must not take these supplements under any circumstances:

- Hypertension
- Diabetes
- Kidney problems
- Heart disease

I’m not saying they shouldn’t be used, only that you should know what you’re taking so you can adjust the doses and get an excellent result.
