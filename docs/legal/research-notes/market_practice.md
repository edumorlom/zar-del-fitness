# Argentine gym and online-coaching legal pages: market practice, enforcement and complaints (as of Nov 2026)

> Method note for the report writer: this session's network policy blocked direct fetching of every company site tried (sportclub.com.ar, megatlon.com, smartfit.com.ar, openpark.com.ar, fiter.com.ar, onfit.com.ar, diariojudicial.com). Everything below comes from search-engine extracts of those pages and documents (WebSearch), not from reading full pages. Short quoted fragments were in the extracts, but **check every clause against the live document before reusing or quoting it**. Footer elements (Data Fiscal QR, Defensa del Consumidor link, cookie banners) could not be seen at all.

## 1. What peers' published T&Cs and Privacy Policies contain (chains, boutique, online)

### Takeaway
The big chains (SportClub, Megatlon, Smart Fit, Fiter, OnFit) publish legal text as **plan-by-plan PDFs, adhesion contracts and FAQ pages** rather than one unified, readable page. The documents are written in dense contractual Spanish ("LA EMPRESA", "el socio"). They cover cancellation and debits in detail but say little about biometrics, retention or health data. Image-rights clauses are broad opt-out grants: Megatlon covers image, voice and "reactions" for advertising, and SportClub has a blanket authorization to capture and reproduce images. No peer was found with a dedicated biometric clause, a stated retention period, or a plain-language summary.

### Inferences
- **Recurring patterns across chains:**
  - Automatic monthly renewal by card or debit.
  - A cancellation cut-off tied to the debit run (Megatlon the 15th; SportClub corporate the 10th; OnFit corporate the 10th).
  - Minimum stays or fixed annual terms with penalties (Fiter 60 days; Smart Fit Black 20%; Megatlon annual 12 months).
  - Unilateral price-change clauses.
  - A health self-declaration or apto médico.
  - A broad image opt-out.
- **Weak spots across chains:**
  - Biometric disclosures (SportClub uses biometrics and QR in practice but has no privacy text on it).
  - Retention periods.
  - Health-data processing.
  - Consistency between app-store data declarations and the policies.
- **Room to stand out:** no peer publishes a short plain-language summary, a single consolidated legal hub, or a bilingual version. Smart Fit's Portuguese FAQ pages on its .com.ar domain show how low the bar is.
- **Clause shapes to avoid** for a premium boutique:
  - "Continued use = acceptance of price change" (SportClub Ushuaia).
  - A blanket image grant including "reactions" (Megatlon).
  - These are the shapes regulators and courts have penalized (Q3) or that conflict with consent-based image law.

- **Full texts not readable:** full documents could not be read because of network blocks. Data-retention periods, international-transfer and cloud-provider clauses, cookie policies and governing-law or jurisdiction clauses were not confirmed for any chain.
- **Missing documents:**

## 2. Mandatory consumer elements displayed (botón de arrepentimiento, botón de baja, ARCA Data Fiscal QR, Defensa del Consumidor link, cookie banner, AAIP legend)

### Takeaway
Only indirect evidence was available, because the sites themselves could not be loaded:
- SportClub's plan PDFs refer to a "botón de arrepentimiento disponible" and allow cancellation through the website, the same channel used to sign up.
- Megatlon's privacy policy carries an AAIP registration reference and contact details, and it hosts an online complaints-book page.
- Several chains still steer cancellation to email, WhatsApp, the front desk or registered letter.
- Whether any chain shows the Data Fiscal QR, a Defensa del Consumidor link or a cookie banner could not be verified.

### Inferences
- **Online cancellation is not universal:** the market has not fully converged on a self-service botón de baja on the home page. Front-desk, registered-letter and notice-period mechanics (Smart Fit, Fiter) remain common.
- **Easy differentiator:** a premium gym that shows a visible "Botón de arrepentimiento" and "Botón de baja" on its home page and in the portal, with instant emailed confirmation and a ticket number, would lead the market.
- **Cancellation before the debit run:** Megatlon's corporate "before the 15th" rule and SportClub's 1–10 window show the industry ties cancellation to the debit date. Stating a clear cut-off and confirming it in writing is the main way to prevent the "charged after baja" complaints in Q4.

### Gaps
- **Not observed:** the presence of the ARCA Data Fiscal QR, a Defensa del Consumidor (Ventanilla Única Federal / CABA) link, cookie banners, the exact AAIP legend text, and literal botón de arrepentimiento or baja buttons on each chain's home page. ## 3. Enforcement: regulator sanctions, court cases and collective actions involving gyms (Argentina, especially CABA)

### Takeaway
Recorded enforcement against Argentine gyms is mostly about:
- **unilateral or uninformed price increases** (art. 4 information duty);
- **service non-performance** (art. 19);
- **unilateral extra charges**.

The most frequent target is Megatlon (Gimnasios Argentinos S.A.), described by a CABA court as a repeat offender ("reincidente"). Fines in the cases found were modest in nominal pesos (ARS 10,000–65,000). Civil liability for injuries can be very large (about ARS 85M in 2026). No AAIP action against an Argentine gym over biometrics or health data was found. Spain's AEPD fingerprint fines are the nearest comparator.

### Cited Findings
- **National Consumer Directorate (DNDC), Disposición 172/18:**
  - It fined Gimnasios Argentinos S.A. **ARS 50,000** under **art. 4 Ley 24.240** for not informing the member in "forma cierta, clara y detallada" about fee increases.
  - The complaint started with automatic debits on a membership.
  - The company argued the member consented by continuing to use the service; this was rejected.
  - The federal administrative appeals court (CNACAF) confirmed the fine (2020).
  - Sources: [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/confirman-la-multa-impuesta-a-un-gimnasio-que-no-informo-el-incremento-de-las-cuotas-del-servicio-ofrecido/25970); [ruling PDF](https://abogados.com.ar/archivos/2020-05-25-105311-gimnasios-argentinos-sa-c-dndc-s-defensa-del-consumidor.pdf); [Estudio Rawson](https://estudiorawson.com.ar/la-importancia-del-deber-de-informacion-multan-a-un-gimnasio-por-omitir-informar-el-aumento-de-la-cuota/)
- **2016 CNACAF rulings against a gym (Megatlon):**
  - The court confirmed a fine for charging a fee higher than agreed. The search extracts give **ARS 10,000** under **art. 19** (failure to provide the agreed service).
  - The contract gave the member a right to cancel if they disagreed with changes.
  - The court found sanctions "no resultan desproporcionadas en función de la posición en el mercado de la empresa sancionada".
  - Sources: [Microjuris, May 2016](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2016/05/04/se-multa-a-un-gimnasio-por-cobrar-una-cuota-superior-a-la-convenida-en-violacion-a-la-ley-de-defensa-del-consumidor/); [Microjuris, Sep 2016](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2016/09/20/se-multa-a-un-gimnasio-por-infraccion-a-la-ley-de-defensa-al-consumidor-al-haber-incurrido-en-incumplimiento-contractual-por-cobrar-una-cuota-superior-a-la-convenida/); [vLex 843684307](https://ar.vlex.com/vid/gimnasios-argentinos-sa-c-843684307); [vLex 609071722](https://ar.vlex.com/vid/gimnasios-argentinos-sa-c-609071722). One search summary instead describes the 2016 fine as being for a clause allowing price changes, plus direct damages; the exact basis should be checked in the rulings.
- **CABA consumer directorate (DGDyPC) v. Megatlon:**
  - DGDyPC fined Megatlon **ARS 65,000** under **art. 19** and ordered **ARS 90,859.44** in direct damages after a member's bike was stolen from the gym's bike rack. The ground was breach of the implied duty of security and custody.
  - Sala II of the CABA administrative and consumer-relations appeals court confirmed, citing the "carácter de reincidente de Gimnasios Argentinos".
  - Source: [Diario Judicial, "La Justicia no lo dejó a pata"](https://www.diariojudicial.com/news-100335-la-justicia-no-lo-dejo-a-pata)
  - Related: [Infobae, 22 Jun 2025](https://www.infobae.com/judiciales/2025/06/22/le-robaron-la-bicicleta-que-dejo-estacionada-mientras-entrenaba-y-condenaron-al-gimnasio-a-pagar-mas-que-su-valor/).
  - Date uncertain: one search dated the Diario Judicial note 31 Jul 2026, and the Infobae piece is June 2025. They may be the same or different proceedings.
- **GEBA c/ DGDyPC (19 Jun 2025, CABA administrative and consumer-relations appeals court):**
  - The court confirmed a DGDyPC fine on Club Gimnasia y Esgrima de Buenos Aires for charging a **unilateral extraordinary fee not provided for in the membership contract**.
  - It held that a sports club is a "proveedor" under Ley 24.240 despite its claim to be governed by Ley 22.315 and its statute.
  - It applied the doctrine of dynamic burdens of proof.
  - Sources: [Microjuris, 21 Oct 2025](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2025/10/21/fallos-multa-un-club-deportivo-cobro-una-cuota-extraordinaria-de-manera-unilateral-y-no-prevista-en-el-contrato-de-membresia/); [Microjuris podcast, Dec 2025](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2025/12/02/podcast-es-un-club-pero-tambien-es-un-proveedor-y-se-le-aplica-la-ley-de-defensa-del-consumidor/)
- **Collective actions:**
  - A consumer association sued a gym chain (Megatlon) in Juzgado Comercial 8 over fees charged during the COVID lockdown. It seeks refunds with interest plus punitive damages; the outcome is not reported — [Justicia de Primera, Oct 2020](https://justiciadeprimera.com/2020/10/31/admiten-reclamo-colectivo-a-cadena-de-gimnasios-por-cobros-en-cuarentena/).
  - ACYMA runs a collective case alleging Megatlon failed to notify fee increases, seeking repayment of the increases — [ACYMA "Juicio MEGATLON"](https://acyma.org.ar/juicio-megatlon/).
- **Injury liability (civil):**
  - In March 2026 a civil appeals court confirmed an indemnity of about **ARS 85 million** against a gym chain for a member's serious accident.
  - It **rejected punitive damages** because no intent or gross negligence was proven.
  - Sources: [Infobae, 23 Mar 2026](https://www.infobae.com/judiciales/2026/03/23/grave-accidente-en-un-gimnasio-condenaron-a-una-cadena-deportiva-a-pagar-una-indemnizacion-millonaria-a-un-socio/); [iProfesional](https://www.iprofesional.com/legales/450857-fallo-clave-gimnasio-pagara-indemnizacion-millonaria-por-accidente-cliente)
- **Fall at a SportClub location:** a damages claim over a fall from a handrail was rejected, with the fall attributed to the member's own conduct — [Microjuris, Sep 2021](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2021/09/20/fallos-relacion-de-consumo-corresponde-rechazar-la-demanda-de-danos-ya-que-la-caida-de-un-pasamanos-en-el-gimnasio-fue-producto-de-su-propio-accionar-y-no-por-una-estructura-deficiente-o-negligenc/)
- **CABA Boletín Oficial, 2026:** the DGDyPC dispositions that surfaced (e.g., 2875/26, 3292/26, 3644/26) concern Ley 941 condominium administrators, not gyms. No 2026 gym-specific DGDyPC disposition was found — [Disp. 2875/DGDYPC/26](https://documentosboletinoficial.buenosaires.gob.ar/publico/ck_PE-DIS-SECGVC-DGDYPC-2875-26-7397.pdf); [Disp. 3644/DGDYPC/26](https://documentosboletinoficial.buenosaires.gob.ar/publico/ck_PE-DIS-SECGVC-DGDYPC-3644-26-7449.pdf)
- **Biometrics, Argentine position:**
  - Practitioner sources disagree on whether biometrics are "sensitive" under Ley 25.326. One says the AAIP (via Res. 4/2019) treats biometrics as sensitive only when they can reveal potentially discriminatory data.
  - Guides recommend offering a non-biometric alternative so that consent is free.
  - A GDPR-style reform bill that would expressly list biometric data as sensitive has not passed.
  - Sources: [abogados.com.ar, "Sistemas biométricos en Argentina hoy"](https://abogados.com.ar/sistemas-biometricos-en-argentina-hoy-status-juridico-y-tendencias/40151); [Rankmi](https://www.rankmi.com/blog/biometria-y-geolocalizacion-para-fichar-aspectos-legales-clave)
- **Comparator (Spain, AEPD):**
  - The Metropolitan gym was fined **€27,000** for requiring a fingerprint for access — [Confilegal, 13 Feb 2024](https://confilegal.com/20240213-el-gimnasio-metropolitan-sancionado-con-27-000-euros-por-pedir-la-huella-dactilar-para-acceder-a-las-instalaciones/).
  - In another case the AEPD held that consent through signing member rules was invalid because "no se ofrece una alternativa distinta real y efectiva al uso de la huella" — [AEPD PS/00002/2018](https://www.aepd.es/documento/ps-00002-2018.pdf).
- **Comparator (Chile, Sernac):** about 3,570 gym complaints in 2025, up 21% year on year. Companies rejected 42% and did not respond to 11.5% — [The Clinic, Oct 2025](https://www.theclinic.cl/2025/10/08/el-gimnasio-cerro-de-un-dia-para-otro-denuncias-contra-gimnasios-aumentan-y-superan-las-3-500-en-2025/)

### Inferences
- **Price-change risk:** the enforcement record makes "uninformed or unilateral price change" the top regulatory risk. Courts reject "continued use = consent".
  - Zar del Fitness's T&C should commit to advance written notice of increases (Megatlon's own T&C say 30 days) with a right to cancel at no cost before the increase applies.
- **Art. 19 duty to perform:** this duty covers ancillary services the gym offers, such as bike racks, lockers and booked sessions. Disclaimers will not neutralize a duty of security the gym takes on.
- **Biometrics and image risk:** absent Argentine gym-specific precedent, the AEPD line (offer a real non-biometric alternative, and use separate explicit consent) is the defensible design for fingerprint access.
- **Punitive damages:** their rejection in the 2026 accident case suggests courts reserve them for intentional or grossly negligent conduct. Uncorrected systemic overbilling after a cancellation is a plausible trigger, though no such gym ruling was found.

### Gaps
- **AAIP:** no sanction against a gym (biometric, health data, marketing photos) was found.
- **National consumer directorate (2022–2026):** no gym fines were found beyond the 2018–2020 Megatlon case. Boletín Oficial (national) listings for gyms were not searched successfully.
- **Missing case details:** the exact date and docket of the Megatlon bike-theft DGDyPC ruling, and the outcomes of the COVID-era and ACYMA collective actions, are unconfirmed.
- **No ruling on cancellation obstacles:** no ruling specifically about refusing or obstructing a gym cancellation (botón de baja non-compliance) was found.

## 4. Common consumer complaints about Argentine gyms

### Takeaway
Complaint patterns repeat across chains and over time:
- debits that continue after a cancellation request (often because the gym insists on in-person cancellation);
- automatic-debit enrolments or card charges not authorized;
- non-response to emails;
- disputes over price increases.

These match the cancellation-window and notice-period mechanics in the chains' own terms.

### Cited Findings
- **Megatlon on tuquejasuma:**
  - Members report continued debits after cancelling. In one case the company said the member should have cancelled in person — [tuquejasuma](https://tuquejasuma.com/megatlon-red-de-clubes/reclamos/megatlon-ladron).
  - A member reports unauthorized monthly card charges after a 6-month membership ended, with only the last fee refunded — [tuquejasuma](https://tuquejasuma.com/megatlon-red-de-clubes/reclamos/copiaron-mis-datos-de-visa-me-cobraron-servicios-no-contratados-y-no-responden).
  - Others report non-response and failed Defensa del Consumidor mediation — [tuquejasuma](https://tuquejasuma.com/megatlon-red-de-clubes/reclamos/megatlone-me-roba-todos-los-meses).
  - The site shows 27 complaints solved and an 11% resolution index (the site's own figure, unverified) — [tuquejasuma Megatlon](https://tuquejasuma.com/megatlon-red-de-clubes).
- **Megatlon, older complaints:** debit and post-cancellation charge complaints go back to 2008–2014 — [justosreclamos blog](https://justosreclamos.wordpress.com/2007/12/20/megatlon-gym/)
- **SportClub on tuquejasuma:** complaints include debits after cancellation and being enrolled in automatic debit without being asked (unverified individual reports) — [tuquejasuma SportClub](https://tuquejasuma.com/sport-club); [tuquejasuma sportclub](https://tuquejasuma.com/sportclub)
- **Fiter:** a branch analysis notes chain-level complaints about difficulty cancelling or stopping automatic debits — [educacionfisica.com.ar](https://educacionfisica.com.ar/gimnasio/fiter-abasto/). A review says WhatsApp cancellation is easier — [top-rated.online](https://www.top-rated.online/cities/Buenos+Aires/place/p/9124597/Fiter)
- **Automatic-debit exit (Infobae, Apr 2026):**
  - Specialists say the consumer law and BCRA rules "obligan al banco a gestionar la baja del servicio si el usuario lo solicita por home banking, sin importar el momento del mes".
  - Consumers should keep the request number for any Defensa del Consumidor claim.
  - Charges after a cancellation must be refunded if the service was not used.
  - Source: [Infobae, 21 Apr 2026](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/04/21/debitos-automaticos-como-salir-que-dice-la-ley-y-que-hacer-si-el-banco-no-responde/)
- **Consumer-rights explainers:** they present cancelling a gym or streaming service as a right "aunque no quieran" — [mariovadillo.com.ar](https://mariovadillo.com.ar/cancelar-gym-streaming-suscripcion-baja/); [A24](https://www.a24.com/trends/como-dar-baja-un-servicio-argentina-vueltas-las-leyes-que-protegen-los-consumidores-n1553467)
- **Price-increase disputes:** the regulator-confirmed cases in Q3 arose from members' complaints about debited increases — [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/confirman-la-multa-impuesta-a-un-gimnasio-que-no-informo-el-incremento-de-las-cuotas-del-servicio-ofrecido/25970)

### Inferences
- **Root cause:** the friction comes from cancellation channels that differ from the sign-up channel (online sign-up, in-person or email cancellation) and from debit-date cut-offs that are not prominent.
- **Remedy for Zar del Fitness:**
  - Same-channel or online cancellation, an instant acknowledgement with a ticket number, and a clear "cancel before day X to avoid next month's charge" line on the pricing page and in the portal.
  - Proactive revocation of the Mercado Pago subscription.
- **Inflation context:** monthly price changes are common in Argentina in 2024–2026. Price-increase notices are therefore a recurring touchpoint, and handling them transparently is a differentiator.

### Gaps
- **No official statistics:** no official 2022–2026 counts of gym complaints from the national consumer agency or CABA (by sector) were found. ADECUA, Consumidores Argentinos and Unión de Usuarios y Consumidores had no gym-specific reports in the results.

## 5. Industry body guidance (Cámara de Gimnasios, UGAR, etc.)

### Takeaway
No public industry-body guidance on contract terms, cancellation, data protection or biometrics for gyms was found. The Cámara de Gimnasios de la Argentina mainly appears in COVID-era protocol work, a CABA sworn-statement template, member advisory services and wage bargaining. On apto físico, CABA practice at large chains is an annual digital sworn statement, while elsewhere it depends on the municipality.

### Cited Findings
- **Cámara de Gimnasios site:** it has a "Declaraciones Juradas C.A.B.A." page, a COVID-era sworn-statement template. It describes membership benefits as economic, legal and commercial advice — [camaradegimnasios.org.ar](https://camaradegimnasios.org.ar/noticias/articulo/declaraciones-juradas-caba)
- **COVID protocols:** the Cámara appeared in pandemic reporting saying its members followed their own protocols — [El Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/negocios/los-gimnasios-esquivan-el-decreto-de-las-restricciones-con-propuestas-anti-covid/)
- **Wage bargaining:** UTEDYC and the Cámara de Gimnasios de Argentina agreed a 9% wage increase in four tranches (2025/2026 paritarias) — [iProfesional](https://www.iprofesional.com/management/442653-paritarias-20252026-cuanto-cobran-los-empleados-de-gimnasios-en-argentina)
- **2020 CABA proposal on apto médico:** a legislator proposed replacing the annual apto médico with a PAR-Q-style sworn statement of fitness for non-competitive activity. Cardiologists criticized it — [Infobae, Jul 2020](https://www.infobae.com/sociedad/2020/07/13/analizaran-eliminar-el-requisito-de-tener-un-permiso-medico-para-ir-al-gimnasio-en-caba/); [Página/12](https://www.pagina12.com.ar/278274-gimnasios-planean-eliminar-los-aptos-medicos/)
- **Current CABA practice:** Megatlon uses a digital "Declaración Jurada de Aptitud Física" requested yearly in CABA — [megatlon.com Dudas](https://megatlon.com/dudas/). SportClub cites Ley 139 (CABA) and 12.329 (PBA) and calls the certificate an essential condition — [SportClub Plan Flex PDF](https://eks-production-01-asociate-imagenes.s3.amazonaws.com/MODELO_NUEVO_2025_TYC_PLAN_FLEX_MENSUAL_WEB.pdf)
- **Fiter:** apto físico required outside CABA only — [fiter.com/AR/condiciones](https://fiter.com/AR/condiciones)
- **Privacy concern:** La Nación framed aptos médicos as caught "entre la invasión a la privacidad y la burocracia" — [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/aptos-medicos-entre-la-invasion-a-la-privacidad-y-la-burocracia-nid2105027/)

### Inferences
- **Apto practice differs:** chains handle CABA differently (Megatlon: sworn statement; SportClub: certificate as an essential condition; Fiter: required only outside CABA). This suggests different readings of the current CABA rule. The legal-framework researcher should confirm the current text and amendments of Ley 139 CABA.
- **Premium positioning:** a premium boutique can stand out by collecting a proper medical certificate plus a PAR-Q, treating it as sensitive health data with restricted access.

### Gaps
- **Industry bodies:** no UGAR (or similar) guidance was found. The Cámara de Gimnasios had no published model contract, cancellation guide or data-protection guidance.
- **Ley 139:** whether the 2020 CABA proposal was enacted, or whether Ley 139 was amended, is unconfirmed here.

## 6. Best-practice takeaways for a premium boutique gym (benchmark-derived)

### Takeaway
The market bar is low. Peers rely on scattered PDFs, contractual jargon, front-desk or email cancellation, broad opt-out image grants and no biometric disclosure. Zar del Fitness can stand out with a consolidated, plain-language, bilingual legal hub. It should treat health data, progress photos and fingerprints as consent-based sensitive data, avoid the clause types regulators have sanctioned, and make withdrawal and cancellation one-click.

### Cited Findings
- **What peers do (baseline):**
  - Clauses are spread across plan PDFs and FAQs — [SportClub PDFs](https://eks-production-01-asociate-imagenes.s3.amazonaws.com/MODELO_NUEVO_2025_TYC_PLAN_FLEX_MENSUAL_WEB.pdf); [Smart Fit FAQ in Portuguese](https://www.smartfit.com.ar/faq/96-cancelamento).
  - Image grants are broad and opt-out only — [Megatlon T&C](https://megatlon.com/terminos-y-condiciones/).
  - Biometric access is used without biometric privacy text — [SportClub Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.muvinai.carnet2&hl=en_US); [SportClub privacy](https://app.sportclub.com.ar/legales/Politicas%20de%20Privacidad.html).
- **Sanctioned clause and practice types to avoid:**
  - Uninformed increases and "continued use = consent" — [DNDC 172/18 via abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/confirman-la-multa-impuesta-a-un-gimnasio-que-no-informo-el-incremento-de-las-cuotas-del-servicio-ofrecido/25970).
  - Unilateral extra charges not in the contract — [GEBA, Microjuris](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2025/10/21/fallos-multa-un-club-deportivo-cobro-una-cuota-extraordinaria-de-manera-unilateral-y-no-prevista-en-el-contrato-de-membresia/).
- **Positive features peers already use (worth matching):**
  - A 10-day withdrawal via a botón de arrepentimiento — [SportClub](https://eks-production-01-asociate-imagenes.s3.amazonaws.com/MODELO_NUEVO_2025_TYC_PLAN_FLEX_MENSUAL_WEB.pdf).
  - Cancellation through the same channel as sign-up — [SportClub](https://eks-production-01-asociate-imagenes.s3.amazonaws.com/MODELO_NUEVO_2025_TYC_PLAN_FLEX_MENSUAL_WEB.pdf).
  - Free cancellation when the price rises — [Megatlon corporate model](https://extranet.ort.edu.ar/recursoshumanos/solicitudMegatlon.doc); [Smart Fit Black](https://www.smartfit.com.ar/files/store/7f06bda0bb7b0a628b5aaaaff1a729133025a543eb75e019860683caeedb.pdf).
  - 30 days' notice of changes to terms — [Megatlon T&C](https://megatlon.com/terminos-y-condiciones/).
  - An explicit "no international transfer" statement — [SportClub privacy](https://app.sportclub.com.ar/legales/Politicas%20de%20Privacidad.html).
  - A statement of AAIP registration — [Megatlon privacy](https://megatlon.com/politicas-de-privacidad/).
  - An annual digital health sworn statement in the app — [Megatlon](https://megatlon.com/dudas/).
  - A short appointment-cancellation window (12 hours) — [Smart Fit Coach](https://www.smartfit.com.ar/coachar).
  - Health-data-as-special-category language — [+Trainer privacy](https://mastrainerapp.com/en/privacy-policy/).
- **Biometric consent with a real alternative (comparator):** non-biometric access is the standard Spain's AEPD enforces — [AEPD PS/00002/2018](https://www.aepd.es/documento/ps-00002-2018.pdf); [Confilegal](https://confilegal.com/20240213-el-gimnasio-metropolitan-sancionado-con-27-000-euros-por-pedir-la-huella-dactilar-para-acceder-a-las-instalaciones/)

### Inferences
These are recommendations derived from the benchmark, not sourced facts.
- **A single "Legales" hub** with: T&C, a Privacy Policy, a separate "Datos de salud, fotos y biometría" notice, a cookie policy and the consumer-rights block. The consumer-rights block holds the botón de arrepentimiento, the botón de baja, the Defensa del Consumidor link, the Data Fiscal QR and the AAIP legend.
  - Each document opens with a 5–8 bullet "Resumen en lenguaje claro".
  - Add a Spanish/English toggle to fit the online USD plan and expat clientele.
  - Show a version number and date (Megatlon's "1.0" is the only versioning seen).
- **Cancellation and debits:**
  - One-click online baja in the portal and on the site, with immediate email acknowledgement and a ticket number.
  - A stated cut-off ("cancel before day X; no charge next month").
  - The Mercado Pago subscription cancelled automatically on baja.
  - No minimum stay on monthly plans (as a contrast to Fiter's 60 days and Smart Fit's or Megatlon's annual lock-ins). If annual or prepaid packs are offered, state the refund formula up front.
- **Price increases:** written notice at least 30 days ahead, the right to cancel free before it applies, and no "continued use = acceptance" language.
- **Apto físico and health data:**
  - Require a medical certificate plus a PAR-Q.
  - Explain the purpose (safe programming), who can see it (the assigned coach only), the retention period, and the right to delete or export it.
  - Keep health data separate from marketing consent.
- **Progress and before/after photos:**
  - Two separate opt-in consents: (a) private progress tracking in the portal and (b) marketing use. Each should be granular (face or no face, channels, duration) and revocable, with a stated takedown time.
  - Never bundle (b) into the T&C, in contrast to the Megatlon and SportClub blanket grants.
- **Fingerprint access:**
  - Explicit, separate consent.
  - An equivalent non-biometric alternative (QR or PIN).
  - Template-only storage, a retention and deletion trigger on baja, and a named vendor.
  - A clear statement on whether data leaves Argentina, to avoid the inconsistencies seen between app-store declarations and policies.
- **Online coaching (ARS/USD plans):** spell out the 10-day withdrawal, what counts as "service started", the refund rules for unused weeks, session-cancellation windows (Smart Fit Coach uses 12 hours), and a health disclaimer that does not try to exclude liability for the gym's own negligence.

### Gaps
- **Unverified effect:** no empirical data (e.g., complaint reduction) shows the effect of plain-language or bilingual legal pages in the Argentine gym market. The recommendations above are reasoned from the benchmark and enforcement patterns, not from measured outcomes.
