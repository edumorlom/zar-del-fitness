## 1. Governing law in Nov 2026: Ley 25.326 and Decreto 1558/2001, the reform bills, and Convenio 108/108+

### Takeaway
Ley 25.326 (2000) and its regulatory Decreto 1558/2001 remain fully in force in November 2026. No new data protection law has been enacted. The 2023 Executive reform bill lost parliamentary status. It was reintroduced on 16 July 2026 as bill 3397-D-2026 (Rossi). Other 2026 bills exist (1751-D-2026, Yeza), but none had a committee report (dictamen) or a floor vote as of late September 2026. Argentina is a party to Convention 108 (Ley 27.483) and has approved and ratified the amending Protocol, Convention 108+ (Ley 27.699). The AAIP (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública) is the supervisory authority.

### Inferences
- Draft the privacy policy and consent flows under Ley 25.326, Decreto 1558/2001 and AAIP resolutions. Optionally build in GDPR-style good practice (portability, breach notification, human review of automated decisions) to future-proof against bill 3397-D-2026 or 1751-D-2026. Do not cite either bill as binding.
- Convention 108+ is approved by an Argentine statute, but the amending Protocol enters into force internationally only once enough Parties ratify. Its stricter rules (biometric data as a special category, art. 7.2 breach notification to the authority) are best treated as persuasive or interpretive, not as directly enforceable obligations on private companies in Nov 2026. The AAIP already uses them in its interpretation (Res. 255/2022).

## 2. Information duties at collection (art. 6), privacy policy contents, and the mandatory AAIP legend (Res. AAIP 14/2018, replacing Disp. DNPDP 10/2008)

### Takeaway
Before collecting data, the business must tell data subjects expressly and clearly the five items in art. 6. The old DNPDP legend (Disp. 10/2008) was **repealed** by AAIP Resolución 14/2018 (6 March 2018, Boletín Oficial 9 March 2018). Res. 14/2018 requires the art. 6 information, with special mention of how rights are exercised, to be shown "en forma clara y expresa, en un sitio visible". Its legend names the AAIP, not the DNPDP, as the control authority.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 6 Ley 25.326 [statutory text]: "Cuando se recaben datos personales se deberá informar previamente a sus titulares en forma expresa y clara: a) La finalidad para la que serán tratados y quiénes pueden ser sus destinatarios o clase de destinatarios; b) La existencia del archivo, registro, banco de datos, electrónico o de cualquier otro tipo, de que se trate y la identidad y domicilio de su responsable; c) El carácter obligatorio o facultativo de las respuestas al cuestionario que se le proponga, en especial en cuanto a los datos referidos en el artículo siguiente [sensitive data]; d) Las consecuencias de proporcionar los datos, de la negativa a hacerlo o de la inexactitud de los mismos; e) La posibilidad del interesado de ejercer los derechos de acceso, rectificación y supresión de los datos." — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)
- Decreto 1558/2001 art. 5 defines informed consent as consent "precedido de una explicación, al titular de los datos, en forma adecuada a su nivel social y cultural, de la información a que se refiere el artículo 6º de la Ley Nº 25.326" — [Decreto 1558/2001, AFIP/ARCA biblioteca](https://biblioteca.afip.gob.ar/dcp/DEC_C_001558_2001_11_29)
- Res. AAIP 14/2018 repealed DNPDP Disposición 10/2008 (15 Sept 2008) and set a new legend in which the AAIP, "en su carácter de Órgano de Control de la Ley N° 25.326, tiene la atribución de atender las denuncias y reclamos". ### Inferences
- Minimum privacy policy and collection-notice content for Zar del Fitness:
  - identity and address of the controller (legal entity or person behind Zar del Fitness; CUIT; Av. Álvarez Thomas 847, Colegiales, CABA)
  - each database or file and its purpose (clients and portal, health and anthropometric evaluation, biometric access, marketing and newsletter, web analytics, AI chat logs, payments)

  - how to exercise access, rectification, update, suppression and marketing opt-out (contact email and address, deadlines)
  - the international transfer disclosure (section 13)
  - the Res. 14/2018 legend in a visible place
- Placement: show the art. 6 notice at each collection point (signup, intake/medical form, fingerprint enrollment, newsletter form, chat widget) with a link to the full policy, not only in the footer. Res. 14/2018 requires the notice before collection and in a visible place.

## 3. Sensitive data: health data and biometric data (fingerprints)

### Takeaway
Health information is expressly "dato sensible" (art. 2). No one can be obliged to provide sensitive data (art. 7.1). Art. 8's health-data permission covers only health establishments and health professionals, so a gym is not covered. The safe position is free, express, informed, **written** and separate consent for each sensitive category, with fields clearly marked optional unless a legal obligation applies. Fingerprints: AAIP Res. 4/2019 (still in force) says biometric data used to identify a person is sensitive only when it can reveal additional, potentially discriminatory information. The AAIP's later reading via Convention 108+ (Res. 255/2022 recitals) treats biometric data as sensitive. Best practice is explicit consent plus a non-biometric alternative (card, PIN, QR).

### Cited Findings
- Art. 2 Ley 25.326 [statutory text]: "Datos sensibles: Datos personales que revelan origen racial y étnico, opiniones políticas, convicciones religiosas, filosóficas o morales, afiliación sindical e información referente a la salud o a la vida sexual." — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)
- Art. 7 Ley 25.326 [statutory text]: "1. Ninguna persona puede ser obligada a proporcionar datos sensibles. 2. Los datos sensibles sólo pueden ser recolectados y objeto de tratamiento cuando medien razones de interés general autorizadas por ley. También podrán ser tratados con finalidades estadísticas o científicas cuando no puedan ser identificados sus titulares. 3. Queda prohibida la formación de archivos, bancos o registros que almacenen información que directa o indirectamente revele datos sensibles…" — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)
- Art. 8 Ley 25.326 [statutory text]: "Los establecimientos sanitarios públicos o privados y los profesionales vinculados a las ciencias de la salud pueden recolectar y tratar los datos personales relativos a la salud física o mental de los pacientes que acudan a los mismos o que estén o hubieren estado bajo tratamiento de aquéllos, respetando los principios del secreto profesional." — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)
- AAIP Res. 4/2019 ("criterios orientadores e indicadores de mejores prácticas", dated 10 Jan 2019, Boletín Oficial 16 Jan 2019). Despite the "orientadores" label, its art. 1 makes it mandatory ("de observancia obligatoria") for everyone covered by the law. On biometrics, the extracts render it as: biometric data that identify a person "no serán nunca considerados datos sensibles a los efectos de la LPDP, salvo que de su análisis surja información cuyo uso pueda resultar potencialmente discriminatorio para su titular". A second extract gives the criterion as covering biometric data "que puedan revelar datos adicionales cuyo uso pueda resultar potencialmente discriminatorio para la persona y que estén dirigidos a identificar de manera unívoca a una persona humana" — [abogados.com.ar on Res. 4/2019](https://abogados.com.ar/mejores-practicas-en-la-aplicacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-resolucion-42019-de-la-agencia-de-acceso-a-la-informacion-publica/23000); [Marval](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/mejores-practicas-en-la-aplicacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-13318); [Microjuris](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2019/01/16/se-aprueban-criterios-orientadores-e-indicadores-de-mejores-practicas-para-la-aplicacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-los-datos-personales/)
- Commentators note that the biometric criterion "no brinda mayores precisiones, dejando varios interrogantes pendientes de resolver" — [abogados.com.ar, "Criterios orientadores…"](https://abogados.com.ar/criterios-orientadores-para-la-interpretacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales/23028)
- Res. AAIP 255/2022 approved a criterion on genetic data. Its recitals cite Res. 47/2018 and Res. 4/2019 (the latter "se expidió en relación a los datos biométricos"). They note that the 108+ framework broadens sensitive data to include genetic and biometric data. No express repeal of the Res. 4/2019 biometric criterion was found — [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/data-technology/argentina-the-data-protection-authoritys-resolution-no-2552022-on-genetic-data); [Beccar Varela, 19 Dec 2022](https://beccarvarela.com/novedades/agencia-de-acceso-a-la-informacion-publica-criterio-orientador-sobre-datos-geneticos/); [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/8209-implemented-resolution-no-2552022-expanding-definition-of-sensitive-data-regarding-genetic-data)
- The AAIP's reform bill expressly expands sensitive data: "Se amplía el concepto de datos sensibles, pasando a incluir datos genéticos y biométricos" (not law) — [AAIP slides on the bill](https://argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/slyt_proyecto_de_ley_de_datos_personales_aaip.pdf)
- No Argentine norm or AAIP decision specific to fingerprints in gyms was found. Comparative only: Spain's AEPD fined a gym €27,000 for requiring fingerprints as the only access method (GDPR arts. 6.1, 9.1, 13). The AEPD's guidance is that consent-based biometric access must offer a non-biometric alternative — [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/que-tu-gimnasio-te-pida-tu-huella-dactilar-excesivo-multa-aepd-no-justificar-bien-su-uso-existiendo-alternativas); [Legálitas](https://www.legalitas.com/actualidad/puede-exigirte-tu-gimnasio-la-huella-dactilar-para-acceder)

Decreto 11/2022 (Anexo I) regulates this as an "autorreporte de salud con carácter de declaración jurada". ### Inferences
- Health data (medical conditions, medications, apto físico or self-report file, and arguably body-composition metrics such as body fat %, visceral fat, BMR and "biological age", plus progress photos) should be treated as sensitive.
  - Collect it with a separate, express, written (electronic) consent checkbox, not pre-ticked and not bundled with the T&Cs.
  - Tell the client which fields are optional (art. 6.c) and what happens if they refuse (art. 6.d).
  - The minimum health self-report CABA requires for gym use is a defensible "legal obligation / general interest authorized by law" basis (arts. 5.2.b and 7.2). Anything beyond that minimum rests on consent.
- Because art. 7.1 says no one can be "obliged" to provide sensitive data, the business should not make portal or online-coaching access conditional on optional health or biometric fields beyond what the law or safety genuinely requires. Where the self-report is legally required for gym access, explain this as the consequence of refusal.
- Fingerprint access:
  - Get express written consent at enrollment.
  - Offer an equivalent non-biometric alternative (card, PIN, QR in portal).
  - Store templates, not images. Minimize, encrypt, and delete templates on membership end.
  - Disclose the reader vendor.
  - This satisfies both readings (Res. 4/2019 "not sensitive unless discriminatory" vs. the 108+ "sensitive" reading).
- Art. 7.3's ban on databases that "reveal sensitive data" is in practice read alongside consent. The safe approach is minimization, segregated storage and access controls for health data.

### Gaps
- The full verbatim text of the Res. 4/2019 biometric criterion was not retrieved. The two extracts word it slightly differently.
- Found no AAIP dictamen or resolution specifically on gym or workplace fingerprint access, and no AAIP guidance specific to health data in fitness or wellness businesses.
- The current consolidated text of CABA Ley 139 after Leyes 6316 and 6616, and whether a medical certificate (as opposed to a self-report) can still be demanded, was not confirmed. Another researcher covering gym regulation should confirm.

## 4. Consent formalities (art. 5 and Decreto 1558/2001 art. 5): written or express consent, digital consent, withdrawal

### Takeaway
Consent must be "libre, expreso e informado". It must be in writing "o por otro medio que permita se le equipare", which covers electronic consent. When it is given together with other declarations it must appear "en forma expresa y destacada", after the art. 6 notice. Consent can be revoked at any time, without retroactive effect. Consent is not needed for data necessary for a contract the data subject is party to (art. 5.2.d), for basic identification lists (art. 5.2.c), or under a legal obligation (art. 5.2.b).

### Cited Findings
- Art. 5.1 Ley 25.326 [statutory text]: "El tratamiento de datos personales es ilícito cuando el titular no hubiere prestado su consentimiento libre, expreso e informado, el que deberá constar por escrito, o por otro medio que permita se le equipare, de acuerdo a las circunstancias. El referido consentimiento prestado con otras declaraciones, deberá figurar en forma expresa y destacada, previa notificación al requerido de datos, de la información descrita en el artículo 6° de la presente ley." — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)
- Art. 5.2 [statutory text]: consent is not necessary when, among other cases, data "b) Se recaben para el ejercicio de funciones propias de los poderes del Estado o en virtud de una obligación legal; c) Se trate de listados cuyos datos se limiten a nombre, documento nacional de identidad, identificación tributaria o previsional, ocupación, fecha de nacimiento y domicilio; d) Deriven de una relación contractual, científica o profesional del titular de los datos, y resulten necesarios para su desarrollo o cumplimiento" — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)
- Decreto 1558/2001 art. 5: informed consent is consent "precedido de una explicación, al titular de los datos, en forma adecuada a su nivel social y cultural, de la información a que se refiere el artículo 6º" — [Decreto 1558/2001, ARCA/AFIP biblioteca](https://biblioteca.afip.gob.ar/dcp/DEC_C_001558_2001_11_29)
- Decreto 1558/2001 art. 5 also provides that consent may be revoked at any time and that revocation has no retroactive effect [statutory text, not re-confirmed in search extracts] — [Decreto 1558/2001, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/70000-74999/70368/norma.htm)

### Inferences
- Electronic click-wrap consent is valid if it is affirmative, separate and prominent ("expresa y destacada"), and logged with timestamp, version of the text, user ID and IP.
- Recommended separate consent toggles:
  1. general T&Cs and privacy notice (acknowledgment)
  2. health data processing (sensitive, written and express)
  3. progress photos
  4. fingerprint enrollment (or choose the alternative)
  5. marketing emails and newsletter
  6. international transfer to the listed providers (section 13)
  7. optional analytics cookies (section 9)
- Withdrawal must be as easy as giving consent (a portal settings page plus an email address). Explain that withdrawing consent to health data may limit personalized training.
- Contract-necessary data (name, email, phone, bookings, payments) can rely on art. 5.2.d. DNI with name and birth date fits art. 5.2.c. Marketing, health and biometric data cannot rely on these exceptions.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve the verbatim revocation sentence of Decreto 1558 art. 5 in-session. Verify on InfoLEG.
- No AAIP resolution specific to the formalities of electronic consent was found beyond the "otro medio que permita se le equipare" wording.

## 5. Registration in the Registro Nacional de Bases de Datos Personales (AAIP)

### Takeaway
Registration remains mandatory under art. 21 for private databases "destinados a dar informes". Decreto 1558/2001 art. 1 reads this broadly as any database that goes beyond exclusively personal use. Registration is done online through TAD (Trámites a Distancia). First the controller ("responsable") is registered, then each database. Registration does not expire but must be updated when the declared information changes.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 21 Ley 25.326 requires public databases, and private databases intended to provide reports, to be registered in the Registro Nacional de Bases de Datos Personales. The procedure runs through TAD — [argentina.gob.ar, Obligaciones de los responsables](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/responsables/obligaciones); [argentina.gob.ar, Trámites ante el Registro](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/tramites)
- Decreto 1558/2001 art. 1 [statutory text]: "private databases intended to provide reports" includes "aquellos que exceden el uso exclusivamente personal y los que tienen como finalidad la cesión o transferencia de datos personales, independientemente de que la circulación del informe o la información producida sea a título oneroso o gratuito" — [Decreto 1558/2001, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/70000-74999/70368/norma.htm)
- Databases kept for exclusively personal use are exempt — [Linklaters, Data Protected – Argentina](https://www.linklaters.com/en/insights/data-protected/data-protected---argentina); [argentina.gob.ar, trámites](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/tramites)
- Procedure:
  - Registering the controller is a prerequisite to registering any database.
  - Access is with Clave fiscal level 2 or higher (AFIP, now ARCA) or a TAD power of attorney. A legal entity may use its own clave fiscal or appoint an individual in TAD.
  - The controller declares its CUIT/CUIL and name.
  - The registration "no tiene vencimiento", but changes must be updated in the Registry.
  - To deregister a controller, all its databases must be deleted first.
  - Contact: registrobasesdedatos@aaip.gob.ar.
  — [argentina.gob.ar, Inscribir un responsable de bases de datos personales privadas](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/servicio/inscribir-un-responsable-de-bases-de-datos-personales-privadas); [AAIP TAD instructivo (bases privadas)](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/instructivo_tad_inscripcionymodificacion_basesprivadas_3.pdf)
- Res. AAIP 145/2025 (30 July 2025; Boletín Oficial 4 Aug 2025) is a programme for national public administration bodies to register their databases. It is not a change for private companies — [Res. 145/2025, argentina.gob.ar](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/resoluci%C3%B3n-145-2025-415899/texto)
- Failure to register is an infraction under the AAIP sanctions regime (Res. 126/2024) — [argentina.gob.ar, trámites](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/tramites); [Marval on Res. 126/2024](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/novedades-en-la-clasificacion-de-infracciones-y-en-el-regimen-de-graduacion-de-sanciones-16832)

### Inferences
- Zar del Fitness (the legal entity or sole proprietor with CUIT) should register as controller and then declare its databases. A plausible set, grouped by purpose:
  1. clients and members (identification, contact, DNI, contract, bookings, payments)
  2. health and physical-evaluation data (medical conditions, medications, apto físico or self-report, anthropometrics, photos), declared with sensitive data
  3. biometric access control (fingerprint templates)
  4. marketing, newsletter and leads (email list, web form subscribers)
  5. optionally, website and analytics or AI-chat logs if they hold identifiable data, plus employees/staff if any
- In each declaration, list the international transfers and processors (Clerk, Railway, Cloudflare, Resend, Google, digui.ai).

### Gaps
- Cost: the public-database version is shown as "Gratuito". The fee for private registration was not confirmed in this session (to my knowledge it is free, but verify on the TAD page).
- No 2024–2026 resolution changing the private-sector registration form was found. Absence was not confirmed exhaustively.

## 6. Data subject rights: access, rectification, update, suppression; how they must be offered; habeas data

### Takeaway
Access must be answered within **10 calendar days** of a reliable request. It is free at intervals of no less than six months unless a legitimate interest is shown (art. 14). It is always free for marketing databases (art. 27.2). Rectification, update and suppression must be carried out within **5 business days** (art. 16). If the controller does not comply, the data subject may file the judicial habeas data action (art. 43 Constitución Nacional; arts. 33 ff. Ley 25.326) or complain to the AAIP. The AAIP sanctions non-response, for example a fine on Rappi for ignoring a deletion request.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 14 [statutory text]: "2. El responsable o usuario debe proporcionar la información solicitada dentro de los diez días corridos de haber sido intimado fehacientemente. Vencido el plazo sin que se satisfaga el pedido, o si evacuado el informe, éste se estimara insuficiente, quedará expedita la acción de protección de los datos personales o de hábeas data prevista en esta ley. 3. El derecho de acceso a que se refiere este artículo sólo puede ser ejercido en forma gratuita a intervalos no inferiores a seis meses, salvo que se acredite un interés legítimo al efecto." — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm); see also [Marval, "Protección de datos personales – derecho de acceso"](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/proteccion-de-datos-personales---derecho-de-acceso-5489)
- Art. 16.3 [statutory text]: the controller "debe proceder a la rectificación, supresión o actualización de los datos personales del afectado… en el plazo máximo de cinco días hábiles de recibido el reclamo del titular de los datos o advertido el error o falsedad". Art. 16.5: suppression does not apply when it could harm legitimate rights or interests of third parties "o cuando existiera una obligación legal de conservar los datos" — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)
- Art. 27.2 [statutory text]: in advertising databases "el titular de los datos podrá ejercer el derecho de acceso sin cargo alguno". Art. 27.3: "El titular podrá en cualquier momento solicitar el retiro o bloqueo de su nombre de los bancos de datos a los que se refiere el presente artículo." — [Marval, "derecho de bloqueo"](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/proteccion-de-datos-personales----derecho-de-bloqueo-5534)
- AAIP enforcement: lack of proof of identity is not sufficient grounds for failing to answer an access request — [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/la-falta-de-acreditacion-de-identidad-no-resulta-suficiente-para-no-contestar-un-pedido-de-acceso-a-datos-personales-2/)
- AAIP fined Rappi ARS 80,000 for not handling a data-suppression request — [IAPP, "Se sanciona a una empresa por no atender el derecho a la supresión"](https://iapp.org/news/a/se-sanciona-a-una-empresa-por-no-atender-el-derecho-a-la-supresion-de-los-datos-personales)
- The AAIP's own privacy policy describes processing "para la gestión de solicitudes, reclamos y denuncias conforme a lo normado en las leyes N° 27.275, 25.326, 26.951", and says users may seek review before the agency as control authority — [AAIP privacy policy](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/politica-de-privacidad-de-la-aaip)
- Res. 4/2019 criterion on automated decisions: on request, the controller should give the data subject an explanation of decisions based solely on automated processing that produce harmful legal effects or significantly affect them — [abogados.com.ar on Res. 4/2019](https://abogados.com.ar/mejores-practicas-en-la-aplicacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-resolucion-42019-de-la-agencia-de-acceso-a-la-informacion-publica/23000)

### Inferences
- The policy should give:
  - a dedicated channel (email plus postal address, and a portal self-service view or export of data)
  - the deadlines: 10 calendar days for access, 5 business days for rectification or suppression
  - the free-access rule (every 6 months; always free for marketing data)
  - the ability to block or remove the client from marketing at any time
  - the right to complain to the AAIP (legend)
- Identity checks should be proportionate (the logged-in portal or a matching email). Missing ID is not a reason to ignore a request.
- Retention exceptions to suppression: payment and invoice records (tax obligations) and the health self-report where CABA rules require it can be kept for the legally required period, citing art. 16.5.

### Gaps
- Habeas data procedural details (court competence, procedure) were not researched in depth. Arts. 33–44 of Ley 25.326 and art. 43 CN apply.
- The CABA-specific habeas data framework (Ley 1845) applies to the CABA public sector and was not researched as applicable to private parties.

## 7. Security measures, breach notification, retention

### Takeaway
Art. 9 requires adequate technical and organizational security. AAIP Res. 47/2018 sets **recommended**, not mandatory, security measures for automated and non-automated processing. It replaced the earlier mandatory regime and recommends notifying incidents to the AAIP (incidente.seguridad@aaip.gob.ar). There is no express statutory duty to notify breaches in Ley 25.326. However, the AAIP has said controllers should have reported proactively (Cencosud), has sanctioned failures to adopt Res. 47/2018 measures, and treats timely notification as mitigating. Retention: data must be destroyed once no longer necessary for the purpose (art. 4.7).

### Cited Findings
- Res. AAIP 47/2018 (Boletín Oficial 25 July 2018) sets recommended security measures. Earlier norms imposed mandatory measures. Res. 47/2018 offers recommendations that "pueden adoptarse o reemplazarse por otras más eficaces" — [Res. 47/2018, Boletín Oficial](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/188654/20180725?busqueda=1); [Marval, "Nueva resolución sobre medidas de seguridad"](https://marval.com/publicacion/nueva-resolucion-sobre-medidas-de-seguridad-y-datos-personales-13216); [Res. 47/2018 copy with annexes](https://consejosalta.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/AAIP-47.pdf)
- Res. 47/2018 recommends notifying security incidents to the AAIP. Annex II gives the address incidente.seguridad@aaip.gob.ar, with at least the nature of the incident, the personal data likely compromised and the immediate corrective actions taken. Per Marval, Res. 332/2020 also suggests notifying data subjects — [Marval, "Fuerza de seguridad es sancionada por un incidente de seguridad"](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/fuerza-de-seguridad-es-sancionada-por-un-incidente-de-seguridad-13517); [AAIP 47 Anexo II](https://consejosalta.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/AAIP-47-ANEXO-II.pdf)
- "La LPDP no establece cuáles son las medidas de seguridad recomendadas y tampoco establece la obligación de notificación de incidentes de seguridad". In the Cencosud case, however, the AAIP concluded that the controller should have proactively reported the incident — [abogados.com.ar, "Multa por Data Breach en Argentina"](https://abogados.com.ar/multa-por-data-breach-en-argentina/29132)
- The AAIP has sanctioned a controller for not adopting Res. 47/2018 measures after an incident (2021 case against a security force) and sanctioned Yahoo over a data breach — [Marval](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/fuerza-de-seguridad-es-sancionada-por-un-incidente-de-seguridad-13517); [abogados.com.ar, "Novedosa sanción a Yahoo por un Data Breach"](https://abogados.com.ar/novedosa-sancion-a-yahoo-por-un-data-breach/24633)
- DLA Piper: breach notification is not specifically required, but notification may be mandatory if the authority specifically requests information on breaches. Failure to report is not in itself a violation, but bears on the effects of the security failure — [DLA Piper, Data Protection Laws of the World – Argentina](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=AR)
- A compliance aggregator claims that a 2020 amendment (Ley 27.401) added breach duties. This conflicts with DLA Piper and the law-firm sources and is unreliable (Ley 27.401 is the 2017 corporate criminal liability law) — [theartofservice](https://compliance.theartofservice.com/frameworks/argentina-law-25-326-personal-data-protection-law); contradicted by [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=AR)
- Art. 4.7 Ley 25.326 [statutory text]: "Los datos deben ser destruidos cuando hayan dejado de ser necesarios o pertinentes a los fines para los cuales hubiesen sido recolectados." Art. 9: the controller must adopt "las medidas técnicas y organizativas que resulten necesarias para garantizar la seguridad y confidencialidad de los datos personales". Art. 10: duty of confidentiality — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)

### Inferences
- The policy can state that the business applies measures in line with AAIP Res. 47/2018:
  - access control and least privilege in the portal (Clerk authentication)
  - encryption in transit and at rest
  - segregation of health and biometric data
  - logging and backups
  - an incident response plan with AAIP notification and client notification when there is risk
- Set and publish retention periods per category, for example:
  - health and evaluation data: deleted N months after the membership or coaching relationship ends
  - fingerprint templates: deleted on end of membership
  - marketing list: until unsubscribe
  - payment records: for the period tax law requires
  - chat logs: short retention
  - analytics: GA4 retention setting

### Gaps
- Res. 332/2020 was not independently verified (single Marval reference).
- No 2024–2026 AAIP resolution creating mandatory breach notification was found.

## 8. Marketing and advertising (art. 27 Ley 25.326, art. 27 Decreto 1558/2001, Disp. DNPDP 4/2009, Ley 26.951 "No Llame"), newsletter consent

### Takeaway
Marketing profiles may use data that the data subject provided or consented to (art. 27.1). The data subject can request removal or blocking at any time (art. 27.3). **Every** advertising communication by email, phone or internet must state, "en forma expresa y destacada", the right to request total or partial removal or blocking (Decreto 1558 art. 27; Disp. 4/2009). Unsolicited advertising must be flagged, and by email the word "publicidad" goes in the subject or header (Disp. 4/2009 art. 2). The No Llame registry (Ley 26.951, run by the AAIP) restricts telephone marketing and must be checked before calling. It is not an email rule.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 27.1 Ley 25.326 [statutory text]: data apt for promotional, commercial or advertising profiles may be processed "cuando éstos figuren en documentos accesibles al público o hayan sido facilitados por los propios titulares u obtenidos con su consentimiento". Art. 27.3: "El titular podrá en cualquier momento solicitar el retiro o bloqueo de su nombre de los bancos de datos a los que se refiere el presente artículo." — [Marval, derecho de bloqueo](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/proteccion-de-datos-personales----derecho-de-bloqueo-5534); [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)
- Decreto 1558/2001 art. 27 (as quoted by Marval): "En toda comunicación con fines de publicidad que se realice por correo, teléfono, correo electrónico, Internet u otro medio a distancia a conocer, se deberá indicar, en forma expresa y destacada, la posibilidad del titular del dato de solicitar el retiro o bloqueo, total o parcial, de su nombre de la base de datos." Marval mistypes the decree number as 1.158/2001 — [Marval](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/proteccion-de-datos-personales----derecho-de-bloqueo-5534)
- Disp. DNPDP 4/2009 (4 March 2009), art. 1: "la opción para el ejercicio del derecho de retiro o bloqueo contemplada en el artículo 27, inciso 3, de la Ley Nº 25.326, deberá aparecer en toda comunicación que se efectúe con fines publicitarios", together with the mechanism to exercise it. Art. 2: "cuando se efectúen envíos de comunicaciones de publicidad directa no requeridas o consentidas previamente por el titular del dato personal, deberá advertirse en forma destacada" that it is advertising, and for email "deberá insertarse en su encabezado el término único 'publicidad'" — [abogados.com.ar, "Ejercicio del Derecho de Retiro o Bloqueo (Opt Out)"](https://abogados.com.ar/ejercicio-del-derecho-de-retiro-o-bloqueo-opt-out/2805); [Marval](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/proteccion-de-datos-personales----derecho-de-bloqueo-5534); [Disp. 4/2009 official text page](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-151221/texto)
- Failing to remove or block a name and email address from advertising databases on request is a typified infraction ("No retirar o bloquear el nombre y dirección de correo electrónico de los bancos de datos destinados a publicidad cuando su titular lo solicite") in the sanctions regime, now consolidated in Res. 126/2024 — [Microjuris on 2015 sanctions reform](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2015/02/24/se-reformula-el-regimen-de-infracciones-y-sanciones-para-conductas-violatorias-de-la-ley-n-25-326-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-y-se-fijan-los-nuevos-niveles-en-la-graduacion-de-las-sancione/); [Marval on Res. 126/2024](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/novedades-en-la-clasificacion-de-infracciones-y-en-el-regimen-de-graduacion-de-sanciones-16832)
- Ley 26.951 Registro Nacional "No Llame":
  - Res. AAIP 126/2024 (Boletín Oficial 24 May 2024) approved a new implementation of the registry at nollame.aaip.gob.ar and unified the infractions register under Leyes 25.326 and 26.951.
  - Telemarketers and advertisers must check the registry before offering goods or services.
  - Infractions are framed as contacts "utilizando los servicios de telefonía en cualquiera de sus modalidades".
  — [abogados.com.ar, "Nuevo procedimiento del Registro Nacional No Llame…"](https://abogados.com.ar/nuevo-procedimiento-del-registro-nacional-no-llame-clasificacion-de-infracciones-y-unificacion-del-registro-de-infractores/34867); [Boletín Oficial 24/05/2024](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/308122/20240524); [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/se-actualiza-en-argentina-el-registro-nacional-no-llame-con-nuevas-clasificaciones-de-infracciones-y-sanciones)
- The AAIP has confirmed sanctions for contacting users registered in No Llame — [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/se-confirma-una-sancion-por-contactar-a-usuarios-inscriptos-en-el-registro-nacional-no-llame/35439)

### Inferences
- Newsletter or broadcast emails to clients:
  - Use an unticked opt-in at signup or portal registration. This makes the email "consented", which avoids the "publicidad" header requirement that applies to unsolicited emails, though adding it does no harm.
  - Include in every email a prominent unsubscribe and blocking notice and link.
  - Honor opt-outs promptly (5 business days by analogy to art. 16).
  - Disclose Resend as the email processor.
- Suggested footer wording, based on the Decreto 1558 art. 27 text: "Usted puede solicitar en cualquier momento el retiro o bloqueo, total o parcial, de su nombre de nuestra base de datos de envíos publicitarios haciendo clic aquí o escribiendo a [email], conforme el art. 27 inc. 3 de la Ley 25.326."
- If the gym markets by phone, SMS or WhatsApp to numbers not supplied with consent, check nollame.aaip.gob.ar first. Calls to existing clients who consented are lower risk, but the registry should still be checked.

### Gaps
- Whether Ley 26.951 and Decreto 2501/2014 cover SMS and WhatsApp messages was not confirmed in this session.
- A "Disposición 17/2010" on opt-out mechanics was mentioned in one search extract but not verified.

## 9. Cookies and web analytics (GA4)

### Takeaway
Argentina has no cookie-specific statute and no AAIP resolution or guide specifically on cookie banners. Cookies and analytics identifiers that are personal data fall under the general rules: art. 6 information and art. 5 consent unless an exception applies. Market practice is to inform in a cookie policy and to seek consent (or at least an opt-out) for non-essential analytics and advertising cookies.

### Cited Findings
- No separate ePrivacy-style law exists, and the AAIP has not published cookie-specific rules — [CookieHub, Argentina PDPA](https://www.cookiehub.com/pdpa-argentina); [Flexyconsent](https://flexyconsent.com/blog/argentina-data-protection-reform-cookie-consent-guide/)
- Template providers claim that analytics, advertising and social cookies require consent and strictly necessary cookies do not. This is market practice, not verified law — [forms-legal, Política de Cookies Argentina](https://forms-legal.com/es/argentina/business/policies/politica-cookies-argentina)
- Baker McKenzie's handbook has an Argentina chapter on "Cookies, Online Tracking and Direct Marketing" (not retrieved) — [Baker McKenzie Resource Hub](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/latin-america/argentina/topics/cookies-online-tracking-and-direct-marketing)

### Inferences
- Defensible approach for zardelfitness.com.ar:
  - Use a cookie notice or banner that loads GA4 only after the user accepts (Google Consent Mode).
  - Use a cookie policy listing GA4 (Google, US transfer), Clerk session cookies (necessary), Cloudflare (necessary or security) and the digui.ai widget.
  - Turn on GA4 IP or data minimization settings and short retention.
- International visitors (online coaching sold in USD) may trigger GDPR or other regimes. Another researcher should check this.

### Gaps
- No official AAIP statement on cookies was found. Baker McKenzie's chapter was not retrieved.

## 10. AI and chatbots (AI receptionist with access to bookings, evaluations and payments)

### Takeaway
There is no binding AI statute. The AAIP created a "Programa de transparencia y protección de datos personales en el uso de la Inteligencia Artificial" (Res. AAIP 161/2023). In 2024 it published a "Guía para entidades públicas y privadas en materia de transparencia y protección de datos personales para una Inteligencia Artificial responsable". The guide recommends data protection impact assessments before deployment, minimization, anonymization or encryption, traceability and auditability, transparency and explainability, and continuous monitoring across the AI life cycle. Ley 25.326 applies fully to personal data the chatbot processes, including health data in evaluations.

### Cited Findings
- Res. AAIP 161/2023 (30 Aug 2023; Boletín Oficial 4 Sept 2023) creates the AI transparency and data protection programme for public and private sectors — [Boletín Oficial, Res. 161/2023](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/293363/20230904); [EY Law alert](https://www.ey.com/es_ar/technical/law/ey-law-alerts-argentina/inteligencia-artificial-programa-transparecia-proteccion-datos)
- The guide was published after a preliminary version with comments open until 30 Oct 2024. Final publication was covered in late Nov 2024. It covers the AI life cycle in four stages from design to post-deployment maintenance. ### Inferences
- The privacy policy should disclose:
  - that an AI assistant (provider digui.ai) answers chats
  - that for logged-in clients it can read bookings, evaluations (health data) and payments
  - the purposes, the retention of chat logs, whether conversations are used to train models (contractually prohibit this where possible), and the international transfer
  - that clients can ask for a human
- Treat digui.ai as a processor (art. 25 Ley 25.326) under a contract with confidentiality, security and no-secondary-use clauses. Model clauses are needed if the data goes to a non-adequate country (section 13).
- Run and document a short impact assessment, as the AAIP guide recommends. Health-data access by the bot should rest on the client's express consent and be minimized (for example, show only what the client asks for, after authentication).

## 11. Minors' data

### Takeaway
Ley 25.326 sets no specific age of digital consent. AAIP Res. 4/2019 includes a criterion on minors' consent based on the progressive autonomy rules of the Código Civil y Comercial (arts. 26 and 639). It requires only "best efforts" to verify that whoever consents for a minor holds parental responsibility. The often-cited age of 13 is a commentator's reading, not an express rule.

### Cited Findings
- Res. 4/2019 recitals: the Código Civil y Comercial "ha receptado en los artículos 26 y 639" the progressive capacity of minors, who may exercise their rights by themselves "conforme con la evolución de sus facultades". It is therefore "necesario establecer criterios orientadores para la obtención del consentimiento de los menores de edad" — [abogados.com.ar on Res. 4/2019](https://abogados.com.ar/mejores-practicas-en-la-aplicacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-resolucion-42019-de-la-agencia-de-acceso-a-la-informacion-publica/23000); [Microjuris](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2019/01/16/se-aprueban-criterios-orientadores-e-indicadores-de-mejores-practicas-para-la-aplicacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-los-datos-personales/)
- "En el caso de menores, solo exige mejores esfuerzos para corroborar que quien preste consentimiento por el menor sea quien tenga la responsabilidad parental." — [Marval](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/mejores-practicas-en-la-aplicacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-13318)
- One commentator suggests that minors from 13 years old could validly consent, with case-by-case maturity assessment below that age. This is an interpretation, not an express rule — [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/mejores-practicas-en-la-aplicacion-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-resolucion-42019-de-la-agencia-de-acceso-a-la-informacion-publica/23000)
- Bill 1751-D-2026 contains an age-verification requirement (not law) — [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/19260-age-verification-requirement-in-bill-providing-regulatory-framework-for-protection-of-personal-data-and-privacy-no-1751-d-2026)

### Inferences
- The portal collects birth year, so the business can detect minors. Because health and biometric data are sensitive:
  - require parental or guardian consent (signed by the holder of parental responsibility) for anyone under 18 for health, photo and biometric processing
  - for under-13s, do not accept the minor's own consent at all
  - state in the policy that services are aimed at adults and that minors need a guardian
- Contracting a gym membership with a minor also raises civil-capacity issues (CCyC). Another researcher should cover T&Cs.

### Gaps
- The verbatim text of the Res. 4/2019 minors criterion (whether it fixes any age) was not retrieved.

## 12. Sanctions regime and AAIP enforcement trends 2023–2026

### Takeaway
Administrative sanctions are set by art. 31 Ley 25.326, now graded by **Res. AAIP 126/2024** (Boletín Oficial 24 May 2024, in force 1 June 2024). Infractions are leves, graves or muy graves, with warnings, suspension, closure or cancellation of the database, and fines from ARS 1,000 to ARS 100,000 per infraction. When one act sanctions several infractions for the same conduct, the total is capped at the scale maximum ×500 (ARS 40M, 45M and 50M). Paying voluntarily within 20 business days halves the fine. Peso amounts are low after inflation, but database suspension, publicity and the infractors register are real risks. Enforcement in 2024–2026 focuses on unanswered rights requests, non-cooperation with AAIP requests, collections or debt practices, and No Llame.

### Cited Findings
- Res. AAIP 126/2024 tiers:
  - leves: up to 2 apercibimientos and/or a fine of $1,000–$80,000
  - graves: up to 4 apercibimientos, suspension of 1–30 days and/or $80,001–$90,000
  - muy graves: up to 6 apercibimientos, suspension of 31–365 days, closure or cancellation of the database and/or $90,001–$100,000
  - voluntary payment within 20 business days gives a 50% reduction
  - it repealed AAIP Res. 12/18, 240/22, 243/19 and 244/22
  — [abogados.com.ar, "Resolución 126/2024 AAIP: cambios en la clasificación de infracciones…"](https://abogados.com.ar/resolucion-1262024-aaip-cambios-en-la-clasificacion-de-infracciones-a-las-leyes-nro-25326-y-26951-y-en-el-regimen-de-graduacion-de-sanciones/34897); [Marval](https://www.marval.com/publicacion/novedades-en-la-clasificacion-de-infracciones-y-en-el-regimen-de-graduacion-de-sanciones-16832); [Res. 126 Annex 2](https://comercioyjusticia.info/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/ANEXO-2-RES-126.pdf); [Boletín Oficial 24/05/2024](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/308122/20240524)
- Cap for multiple fines for the same conduct in one sanctioning act: "un tope al monto total de la multa por dichas conductas equivalentes al máximo de la escala que corresponda según la gravedad de las infracciones cometidas, multiplicado por quinientos (500)". Beccar Varela gives ARS 40,000,000 (leves), 45,000,000 (graves) and 50,000,000 (muy graves), which matches 80,000, 90,000 and 100,000 ×500 — [Beccar Varela, 28 May 2024](https://beccarvarela.com/novedades/ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-y-ley-registro-no-llame-modificacion-del-regimen-sancionatorio/); [Beccar Varela (EN)](https://beccarvarela.com/en/novedades/data-protection-law-and-do-not-call-law-modifications-to-the-sanctioning-regime/)
- Grading criteria include harm caused, economic benefit to the offender, recidivism and volume of data processed — [ciberseguridadlatam](https://ciberseguridadlatam.com/argentina-nueva-reglamentacion-sobre-infracciones-y-sanciones-en-materia-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales/)
- Res. AAIP 179/2025 modified the infractors register to speed up sanctions. Reportedly it did not change the fine ranges — [Comercio y Justicia](https://comercioyjusticia.info/leyes-y-comentarios/datos-personales-optimizan-el-registro-de-infractores-para-agilizar-las-sanciones/); [leydeprotecciondedatos.com](https://leydeprotecciondedatos.com/argentina/multas/)
- Recent enforcement:
  - On 2 Nov 2026 the AAIP announced suspension of the database of 5 ON LINE S.R.L. for irregular processing in debt collection (investigation opened April 2026 after complaints). It warned that obligations reach both collection agencies and companies that hand over data to them.
  - It also sanctioned another company for failing to supply information the AAIP requested during an art. 16 suppression investigation.
  — [argentina.gob.ar, "La AAIP sancionó a empresas por incumplimientos a la Ley N° 25.326"](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/la-aaip-sanciono-empresas-por-incumplimientos-la-ley-ndeg-25326)
- The Rappi fine (ARS 80,000) for not honoring a deletion request — [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/se-sanciona-a-una-empresa-por-no-atender-el-derecho-a-la-supresion-de-los-datos-personales); see also [IAPP, "Últimas sanciones…"](https://www.iapp.org/news/a/ultimas-sanciones-de-la-autoridad-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-en-argentina)
- 2022 AAIP annual report figure: 52 sanctioning resolutions with fines of about ARS 7.4 million (secondary source) — [xmslatam](https://xmslatam.com/ar/ley-25326-proteccion-datos-personales/)
- Criminal exposure: Ley 25.326 art. 32 added Código Penal arts. 117 bis and 157 bis (false information in databases; unlawful access to or disclosure of protected databases) [statutory text] — [Ley 25.326, InfoLEG](http://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64790/texact.htm)

### Inferences
- The realistic risks for a small gym are:
  - complaints from clients or ex-clients (unanswered access or deletion requests, marketing after opt-out)
  - an AAIP order to suspend a database
  - civil damages and habeas data suits
  - reputational harm
  Fine amounts are modest, but several infractions can stack up to the ×500 cap.
- The most-enforced items map directly to the policy: rights-handling deadlines, opt-out in marketing, answering AAIP requests, registration and security.

## 13. International transfers (Railway, Cloudflare, Resend, Clerk, Google, digui.ai hosting abroad)

### Takeaway
Art. 12 of Ley 25.326 prohibits transfers to countries without adequate protection, subject to exceptions. Under Decreto 1558 art. 12 the prohibition does not apply where the data subject **expressly consents** or where adequate safeguards exist in a contract (model clauses). The AAIP's adequacy list (Disp. DNPDP 60-E/2016, updated by Res. AAIP 34/2019) covers the EU/EEA and some others. US providers require consent or model clauses: the 2016 AAIP clauses (controller-to-controller and controller-to-processor) or the Ibero-American Network (RIPD) clauses approved by Res. AAIP 198/2023.

### Cited Findings
- Disp. DNPDP 60-E/2016 (Boletín Oficial 18 Nov 2016) approved two sets of model clauses for international transfers: controller-to-controller and controller-to-processor. Parties may use them or a different contract that reflects the same principles and safeguards. Disp. 60/2016 and Res. AAIP 34/2019 set out the countries considered adequate, including the EU member states — [Boletín Oficial 18/11/2016](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/154249/20161118); [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/nueva-regulacion-sobre-transferencias-internacionales-de-datos-personales/33745); [argentina.gob.ar, transferencias internacionales](https://argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/transferencias-internacionales)
- Res. AAIP 198/2023 approved the RIPD model contractual clauses for transfers to non-adequate countries. They complement and do not replace the 2016 clauses, which remain in force — [Marval, "Transferencia internacional de datos: nuevas cláusulas modelo"](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/transferencia-internacional-de-datos-nuevas-clausulas-modelo-15673); [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/argentina-implementa-nuevas-clausulas-contractuales-modelo-para-la-transferencia-internacional-de-datos); [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/investigations-compliance-ethics/argentina-aaip-approves-use-of-iadpn-model-contractual-clauses-for-international-transfers-of-personal-data); [Allende & Brea, 19 Oct 2023](https://allende.com/privacidad-y-ciberseguridad/la-agencia-de-acceso-a-la-informacion-publica-aaip-aprueba-nuevas-clausulas-contractuales-modelo-ccm-para-transferencias-internacionales-10-19-2023/)
- No additional measures are required for a transfer to a non-adequate jurisdiction when the data subject has consented — [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/nueva-regulacion-sobre-transferencias-internacionales-de-datos-personales/33745)

### Inferences
- Disclose each provider and its country (mostly the US) in the privacy policy.
- Obtain express consent to international transfer at signup, as a separate line given that health data is involved.
- Also sign or accept DPAs incorporating the AAIP 2016 or RIPD clauses where vendors allow it. Many US SaaS vendors offer only GDPR SCCs, so consent is the practical primary basis.
- Note the art. 25 processor rules: processors may not use the data for other purposes and must destroy it at the end of the service.

### Gaps
- The search did not confirm the current adequacy list. To my knowledge the US is not on it, but this was not verified in this session.
- Whether a GDPR SCC-based DPA is accepted as "equivalent" by the AAIP was not confirmed.
