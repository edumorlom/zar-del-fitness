## 1. Ley 24.240 (Defensa del Consumidor): current status after DNU 70/2023 and 2024–2026 changes; provisions that matter for services contracted online

### Takeaway
I found no DNU or law in 2024–2026 that changes the **text** of Ley 24.240's substantive articles. The 2026 changes are regulatory: a new abusive-clause list (Disp. 377/2026), new rules on fines (Decreto 377/2026), and the new button regime (Disp. 954/2025). For an online-sold service the key articles are still arts. 4, 10, 10 ter, 10 quater, 19, 33–34, 37 and 38. Art. 38 has a specific website duty: **publish the model adhesion contract on the website**.

### Cited Findings
- **Status/amendments:** Searches for 2025–2026 changes found **no DNU modifying the text of Ley 24.240**. The recent changes found are regulatory: Decreto 377/2026, published in the Boletín Oficial on 22-05-2026, modifies the *reglamentación* of the law. Per its summary, it adds an inciso c) to art. 45 of the reglamentación that lets an offender pay 50% of a fine within 10 business days if it does not appeal. It takes effect on publication — [BO Decreto 377/2026](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/342265/20260522); [argentina.gob.ar resumen](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-426028); [Liga del Consorcista](https://ligadelconsorcista.org/modificacion-de-la-reglamentacion-de-la-ley-de-defensa-del-consumidor-24240-multas-dcto-377-2026)
- The official "normas que modifican" index for Ley 24.240 also lists 2026 provisions on the Registro Nacional de Infractores (RENAI) and on consumer arbitration tribunals. Their content was not visible — [argentina.gob.ar – normas que modifican Ley 24.240](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-24240-638/normas-modifican)
- Earlier amendments still in force: Ley 26.361 (2008) rewrote many articles and **added art. 8 bis and art. 10 ter** — [Ley 26.361 texto](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-26361-139252/texto). **Art. 4 was replaced by Ley 27.250 (2016)** — [consolidated text, argentina.gob.ar](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/638/actualizacion). Ley 27.701 (Budget 2023), art. 119, replaced **art. 47 (sanctions/fines)** — [Marval](https://www.marval.com/Publicacion/ley-de-presupuesto-nacional-2023-se-aprobaron-nuevas-modificaciones-a-la-ley-de-defensa-del-consumidor-15361?lang=es); [vLex](https://vlex.com/vid/ley-presupuesto-nacional-2023-925151940)
- **Art. 10 ter ("Modos de Rescisión"), confirmed by search extracts:** a service contracted by phone, electronically or similar can be cancelled by the consumer "mediante el mismo medio utilizado en la contratación". The company "deberá enviar sin cargo al domicilio del consumidor o usuario una constancia fehaciente dentro de las 72 horas posteriores a la recepción del pedido de rescisión" — [Santa Fe consolidated text](https://www.santafe.gov.ar/index.php/web/content/download/176785/866454); [Juristeca CABA](https://juristeca.jusbaires.gob.ar/?p=2477)
- Full wording of art. 10 ter, from the official consolidated text (not re-fetched): "Cuando la contratación de un servicio, incluidos los servicios públicos domiciliarios, haya sido realizada en forma telefónica, electrónica o similar, podrá ser rescindida a elección del consumidor o usuario mediante el mismo medio utilizado en la contratación. La empresa receptora del pedido de rescisión del servicio deberá enviar sin cargo al domicilio del consumidor o usuario una constancia fehaciente dentro de las SETENTA Y DOS (72) horas posteriores a la recepción del pedido de rescisión. Esta disposición debe ser publicada en la factura o documento equivalente que la empresa enviare regularmente al domicilio del consumidor o usuario." — [argentina.gob.ar Ley 24.240 actualizada](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/638/actualizacion)
- Art. 10 ter is enforced in CABA. Juristeca reports a CABA case confirming an **ARS 80,000 fine** on a telephone company for breaching art. 10 ter, because it did not let the user cancel by the "mismo medio" — [Juristeca CABA](https://juristeca.jusbaires.gob.ar/?p=2477)
- **Art. 10 quater** ("Prohibición de cobro"), from the official consolidated text (not re-fetched): "Se prohíbe el cobro de preaviso, mes adelantado y/o cualquier otro concepto, por parte de los prestadores de servicios, incluidos los servicios públicos domiciliarios, en los casos de solicitud de baja del mismo realizado por el consumidor ante el proveedor del bien o servicio que los consumidores hubieran contratado." A professional forum also points to a ban on charging notice or a month in advance on cancellation — [argentina.gob.ar Ley 24.240 actualizada](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/638/actualizacion); [Portal de Abogados forum](https://portaldeabogados.com/foros/viewtopic.php?t=254617)
- **Art. 4 (information), Ley 27.250 version**, from the official text (not re-fetched): the provider must give "en forma cierta, clara y detallada todo lo relacionado con las características esenciales de los bienes y servicios que provee, y las condiciones de su comercialización". Information must be free and on physical support, unless the consumer expressly opts for another medium the provider offers — [argentina.gob.ar consolidated text](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/638/actualizacion)
- Other articles relevant to online-sold services, from the official text (not re-fetched) — [argentina.gob.ar consolidated text](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/638/actualizacion):
  - **Art. 7:** an offer to indeterminate consumers binds the offeror.
  - **Art. 8:** the details in advertising are part of the contract.
  - **Art. 8 bis:** trato digno.
  - **Art. 10:** a written contract must be "en idioma castellano, en forma completa, clara y fácilmente legible, sin reenvíos a textos o documentos que no se entreguen previa o simultáneamente". It must state the provider's name and address, price and payment conditions, and the final price including extra costs.
  - **Art. 19:** service providers must respect "los términos, plazos, condiciones, modalidades, reservas y demás circunstancias conforme a las cuales hayan sido ofrecidos, publicitados o convenidos".
  - **Art. 33:** a distance sale is one where the offer and acceptance travel by post, telecommunications, electronic or similar means. "No se permitirá la publicación del número postal como domicilio" (a PO box cannot be published as the address).
  - **Art. 34:** 10 calendar days to revoke a sale outside the business premises or at a distance, counted from delivery or the contract, whichever is later, "sin responsabilidad alguna". The right cannot be waived. The seller must report it in writing "en forma clara y notoria" in every sale document.
  - **Art. 37:** these clauses "se tendrán por no convenidas": (a) those that "desnaturalicen las obligaciones o limiten la responsabilidad por daños"; (b) those that waive or restrict consumer rights or widen the provider's rights; (c) those that reverse the burden of proof. The contract is read in the sense most favourable to the consumer.
  - **Art. 38 (as amended in 2016):** anyone who sells to consumers through adhesion contracts "deben publicar en su sitio web un ejemplar del modelo de contrato a suscribir". They must hand a copy free in their premises on request, and display a sign with the wording: "Se encuentra a su disposición un ejemplar del modelo de contrato que propone la empresa a suscribir al momento de la contratación".
- Court practice on art. 4 information duties for gyms: see section 9 (Gimnasios Argentinos S.A. fined ARS 50,000 for not clearly reporting fee increases) — [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/confirman-la-multa-impuesta-a-un-gimnasio-que-no-informo-el-incremento-de-las-cuotas-del-servicio-ofrecido/25970)

### Inferences
- Art. 10 ter covers the Premium plan, which is bought online in the portal. It also covers the WhatsApp-contracted plans, since WhatsApp is plainly an "electrónica o similar" medium. So the T&C must let clients cancel **through the portal/website and through WhatsApp**, and must promise a written cancellation receipt within 72 hours. Requiring in-person cancellation at the gym for a plan contracted by WhatsApp or online is exactly the conduct that art. 10 ter sanctions.
- Art. 10 quater: the T&C must not charge a "preaviso", a "mes adelantado" or any cancellation fee.
- Art. 38: put the full T&C / membership contract on the website as a model contract, and display the statutory sign in the gym in Colegiales.
- Art. 10 and Arts. 4/10: the T&C must be in Spanish and self-contained, with no references to documents that are not handed over.

From background knowledge only, not verified here: DNU 70/2023 amended CCyC rules on contractual freedom and foreign-currency obligations (arts. 765/766, 958, 960), which supports pricing in USD. Verify on InfoLEG.
- The source law and date of art. 10 quater could not be confirmed (believed to be a 2016 amendment).

- Webdecontadores (May 2026) mentions changes to the Ventanilla Única Federal and the "Defensor del Cliente", but cites no norm numbers — [webdecontadores](https://webdecontadores.com.ar/2026/05/22/ley-de-defensa-al-consumidor-modificaciones/)

## 2. Código Civil y Comercial: consumer contracts (1092–1122), contracts at a distance (1105–1116), revocation, abusive clauses (988, 1117–1122), jurisdiction (2654)

### Takeaway
The CCyC gives a **10-day, non-waivable revocation right** for distance or electronic contracts (art. 1110). The right must be shown in highlighted text (art. 1111), and using it costs the consumer nothing (art. 1115). Its exceptions (art. 1116) cover only custom or perishable goods, unsealed or downloaded digital content, and newspapers. **There is no "service already started" exception as in the EU.** Any clause choosing a forum other than the consumer's is void (arts. 1109, 2654).

### Cited Findings
- **Art. 1116, confirmed partly by search:** "Excepto pacto en contrario, el derecho de revocar no es aplicable a los siguientes contratos: …". The list includes digital files "suministrados por vía electrónica, susceptibles de ser descargados o reproducidos con carácter inmediato para su uso permanente" and "c) los de suministro de prensa diaria, publicaciones periódicas y revistas" — [codigocivilonline art. 1116](https://codigocivilonline.com.ar/articulo-1116/); [leyfacil](https://leyfacil.com.ar/?p=3801)
- **Art. 1115, confirmed by search:** using the revocation right must not cost the consumer anything — [codigocivilonline](https://codigocivilonline.com.ar/?p=2491)
- **Art. 1110**, from the official CCyC text (not re-fetched): "En los contratos celebrados fuera de los establecimientos comerciales y a distancia, el consumidor tiene el derecho irrenunciable de revocar la aceptación dentro de los diez días computados a partir de la celebración del contrato… Las cláusulas, pactos o cualquier modalidad aceptada por el consumidor durante este período que tengan por resultado la imposibilidad de ejercer el derecho de revocación se tienen por no escritos." The search tool's own summary agrees: 10 days from contract for services — [InfoLEG CCyC](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/235000-239999/235975/norma.htm)
- **Art. 1111**, from the official text (not re-fetched): the provider must report the right "mediante su inclusión en caracteres destacados en todo documento que presenta al consumidor en la etapa de negociaciones o en el documento que instrumenta el contrato concluido, ubicada como disposición inmediatamente anterior a la firma". "El derecho de revocación no se extingue si el consumidor no ha sido informado debidamente sobre su derecho." — [InfoLEG CCyC](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/235000-239999/235975/norma.htm)
- **Art. 1116 full list**, from the official text (not re-fetched): (a) products made to the consumer's specifications, clearly personalised, or that cannot be returned or deteriorate quickly; (b) sound or video recordings, discs and software unsealed by the consumer, and downloadable files; (c) daily press and periodicals. **No exception for services whose performance has begun** — [InfoLEG CCyC](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/235000-239999/235975/norma.htm)
- **Arts. 1105–1109**, from the official text (not re-fetched) — [InfoLEG CCyC](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/235000-239999/235975/norma.htm):
  - **Art. 1105:** defines distance contracts (concluded exclusively by distance means, including electronic ones).
  - **Art. 1106:** electronic support satisfies any "por escrito" requirement.
  - **Art. 1107:** when electronic means are used, the provider must report, besides the minimum contract content and the right to revoke, "todos los datos necesarios para utilizar correctamente el medio elegido, para comprender los riesgos derivados de su empleo, y para tener absolutamente claro quién asume esos riesgos".
  - **Art. 1108:** electronic offers stay valid while accessible. "El oferente debe confirmar por vía electrónica y sin demora la llegada de la aceptación."
  - **Art. 1109:** the place of performance is where the consumer received or should have received the service. "Ese lugar fija la jurisdicción aplicable… La cláusula de prórroga de jurisdicción se tiene por no escrita."
- **Abusive clauses**, from the official text (not re-fetched) — [InfoLEG CCyC](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/235000-239999/235975/norma.htm):
  - **Art. 985 (adhesion contracts):** the wording must be "clara, completa y fácilmente legible". Clauses that refer to documents not provided beforehand are treated as not agreed, and "la presente disposición es aplicable a la contratación telefónica, electrónica o similares".
  - **Art. 988:** clauses that denature the drafter's obligations, waive or restrict the adherent's rights, or are not reasonably foreseeable are treated as not written.
  - **Art. 1117:** arts. 985–988 apply to consumer contracts.
  - **Art. 1119:** a clause is abusive if it "tiene por objeto o por efecto provocar un desequilibrio significativo entre los derechos y las obligaciones de las partes, en perjuicio del consumidor", whether negotiated or not.
  - **Art. 1121:** the price–service relationship itself cannot be declared abusive.
  - **Art. 1122:** judicial control; the abusive clause is treated as not agreed.
- **Art. 1098**, from the official text (not re-fetched): providers "no pueden establecer diferencias basadas en pautas contrarias a la garantía constitucional de igualdad, en especial, la de la nacionalidad de los consumidores" — [InfoLEG CCyC](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/235000-239999/235975/norma.htm)
- **Art. 2654 (international consumer jurisdiction)**, from the official text (not re-fetched): the consumer may sue in several fora (place of contract, place of performance, defendant's domicile, place where the consumer took steps to contract). The provider may sue the consumer only in the State of the consumer's domicile. "En esta materia no se admite el acuerdo de elección de foro." **Art. 2655** sets the law applicable to international consumer contracts: the consumer's domicile law in the listed cases, such as an offer or advertising directed to that State — [InfoLEG CCyC](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/235000-239999/235975/norma.htm)
- Disp. 377/2026 also lists as abusive any clause making actions follow a jurisdiction other than "el lugar del domicilio del consumidor al tiempo de la celebración del contrato". The exception is an action brought in the consumer's real domicile at the time of filing — [search extract of Disp. 377/2026 analyses](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/disposici%C3%B3n-377-2026-423801/texto)

### Inferences
- The Premium plan (online) and the WhatsApp-contracted plans are distance contracts. The client can revoke within 10 days of contracting and get a refund with no deduction (arts. 1110, 1115; Ley 24.240 art. 34). T&C wording like "no refunds once the pass has started" is risky: art. 1116 has no service exception, and art. 1110 voids clauses that make revocation impossible. Whether a provider may keep payment for days of the service actually used is not settled in the sources found; see Gaps.
- The revocation notice should sit in highlighted text **immediately before the "Pagar"/acceptance button** in the portal (art. 1111). The provider should also send an electronic confirmation of the purchase without delay (art. 1108).
- Forum clause: state that disputes go to the courts of the consumer's domicile, or simply omit any forum clause. Do not impose CABA courts on international USD clients (arts. 1109, 2654). Argentine law cannot be assumed to govern foreign consumers (art. 2655).
- Pricing the international plan in USD should be justified by the client's **residence/location**, not nationality (art. 1098).

### Gaps
- The CCyC article texts could not be re-fetched in this session; the quotes come from the official text as known and should be checked against InfoLEG.
- I found no case law or authority guidance (2024–2026) on prorated refunds when an Argentine consumer revokes within 10 days after using some days of a gym or coaching service.

## 3. "Botón de arrepentimiento": Res. SCI 424/2020 — still in force? Exact requirements

### Takeaway
**Res. 424/2020 is no longer in force.** It was **repealed and replaced by Disposición 954/2025** of the Subsecretaría de Defensa del Consumidor y Lealtad Comercial (Ministerio de Economía), published in the Boletín Oficial on 4-Sep-2025 (BO N° 35.742). Disp. 954/2025 requires a "BOTÓN DE ARREPENTIMIENTO" link "a simple vista, en lugar destacado y en el primer acceso". It must work with no prior registration or extra steps, and the provider must give a code within 24 hours. The adaptation period of 60 calendar days ran out around **4-Nov-2025**, so the rule is fully enforceable now.

### Cited Findings
- Disp. 954/2025 **repeals Res. 316/2018 and Res. 424/2020**. Providers selling at a distance must add two visible links to their site or digital channel: "Botón de arrepentimiento" and "Botón de baja de servicio" — [search extract; law-firm summaries](https://blog.aconpy.com/2025/09/05/ejercicio-del-derecho-de-arrepentimiento-y-la-baja-de-servicios-contratados-a-distancia/); [Tavarone](https://tavarone.com/obligatoriedad-de-implementar-el-boton-de-baja-y-el-boton-de-arrepentimiento-en-canales-digitales-comerciales/); [Nicholson y Cano](https://nicholsonycano.com.ar/alertas-legales/disposicion-ministerio-de-economia-954-2025-nueva-reglamentacion-sobre-boton-de-arrepentimiento-y-boton-de-baja-de-servicio/)
- Official text: [argentina.gob.ar – Disposición 954/2025 texto original](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-417152/texto); PDF copies at [Consejo Salta](https://www.consejosalta.org.ar/wp-content/uploads/DISPO.-954.pdf) and [FAEVYT](https://www.faevyt.org.ar/easyarticles/images/users/1624/DISPOSICIN-954.pdf)
- Publication date: sources differ between 3 and **4 September 2025 (BO N° 35.742)**. It took effect from publication — [search extract](https://contadoresenred.com/ejercicio-del-derecho-de-arrepentimiento-y-la-baja-de-servicios-contratados-a-distancia-disposicion-954-2025/); [Infobae 04-09-2025](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2025/09/04/implementaron-cambios-el-regimen-de-cancelacion-de-compras-online/); [Ámbito](https://www.ambito.com/economia/el-ministerio-dispuso-cambios-la-cancelacion-compras-online-y-bajas-servicios-n6186388)
- Requirements:
  - The link must be "a simple vista, en lugar destacado y en el primer acceso".
  - "El proveedor no podrá requerir al consumidor registración previa ni ningún otro trámite adicional."
  - Within **24 hours** of the request, the provider must answer through the same medium with an identification or management code, and take steps to carry out the revocation.
  - Source: [search extract of Disp. 954/2025 text](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-417152/texto); [iProfesional](https://www.iprofesional.com/legales/436661-entradas-y-servicios-empresas-deben-implementar-el-boton-de-arrepentimiento-y-de-baja)
- Exclusions per one law-firm summary: perishables, products already used or consumed, purchases for resale or productive integration, and the CCyC art. 1116 cases — [RCTZZ](https://rctzz.com.ar/es/insights/%F0%9F%93%9C-evolucion-normativa-del-lboton-de-arrepentimientor-y-nuevas-obligaciones-para-proveedores.disposicion-954-2025)
- Adaptation: 60 calendar days from entry into force, so the new duties are enforceable **from 4 November 2025** — [Nicholson y Cano](https://nicholsonycano.com.ar/alertas-legales/disposicion-ministerio-de-economia-954-2025-nueva-reglamentacion-sobre-boton-de-arrepentimiento-y-boton-de-baja-de-servicio/)
- **Disposición 3/2026** of the Dirección Nacional de Defensa del Consumidor y Arbitraje del Consumo (BO 6-Feb-2026) is described by one commercial guide as complementing Disp. 954/2025. Its content was not found — [search extract](https://markco.digital/blog/boton-de-arrepentimiento-y-boton-de-baja-que-exige-la-disposicion-9542025)
- A 2026 blog post is titled "Botón de baja en la web, tienen que verificar la identidad", which suggests a 2026 rule or interpretation on identity checks. The content could not be accessed — [Derecho en Zapatillas 2026](https://www.derechoenzapatillas.com/2026/boton-de-baja-verificar-identidiad/)
- History (replaced): Res. SCI 424/2020 created the original button ("Botón de arrepentimiento" link on the first screen, no registration, code within 24 hours) — [search extract](https://estudioalonsocarli.com.ar/derecho-de-arrepentimiento-y-boton-de-baja-que-cambio-a-quien-alcanza-y-como-usarlo-sin-dolores-de-cabeza/)

### Inferences
- zardelfitness.com.ar sells the Premium plan online, so it must show a link labelled "BOTÓN DE ARREPENTIMIENTO" on the **home page** (first access), visible without scrolling or hidden menus. It must open a form that does **not** require logging in to the client portal. The site must send an acknowledgment code within 24 hours, then process the refund through Mercado Pago. A link in the footer on every page plus a prominent one on the home page is the conservative design.
- The WhatsApp-contracted plans are not sold "through the website", but as distance contracts they carry the same 10-day right. The button may serve them too.

- The verbatim article numbers and wording of Disp. 954/2025 were not visible; only search extracts were. Verify the label wording ("BOTÓN DE ARREPENTIMIENTO" in capitals, as reported for "BOTÓN DE BAJA DE SERVICIO").
- The content of Disp. 3/2026 and any 2026 identity-verification rule are unknown.

## 4. "Botón de baja": requirements, scope, status

### Takeaway
The botón de baja was first imposed by **Res. 316/2018**, then extended by Res. 271/2020 and Res. 357/2021. It is **now governed by Disp. 954/2025**, which repealed Res. 316/2018. The rule requires a "BOTÓN DE BAJA DE SERVICIO" link "a simple vista, en lugar destacado y en el primer acceso", with no registration and a reply with a code within 24 hours. It sits alongside the statutory duties in art. 10 ter (same medium; written receipt within 72 hours) and art. 10 quater (no charges on cancellation).

### Cited Findings
- Disp. 954/2025: "Los proveedores que comercialicen bienes y servicios a distancia, a través de páginas web y/o canales digitales de comercialización o formato similar, deberán tener a simple vista, en lugar destacado y en el primer acceso, un link denominado 'BOTÓN DE BAJA DE SERVICIO'". The provider "no podrá requerir al consumidor registración previa ni ningún otro trámite adicional". Within 24 hours of the request it must answer by the same medium (art. 5 per search extract) — [search extract](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-417152/texto); [iProfesional](https://www.iprofesional.com/legales/436661-entradas-y-servicios-empresas-deben-implementar-el-boton-de-arrepentimiento-y-de-baja)
- Repeal: Disp. 954/2025 repealed Res. 316/2018 (the earlier botón de baja) and Res. 424/2020 — [search extract](https://tuwebfacil.com.ar/blog/que-tiene-que-tener-tu-web-por-ley/); [Tavarone](https://tavarone.com/obligatoriedad-de-implementar-el-boton-de-baja-y-el-boton-de-arrepentimiento-en-canales-digitales-comerciales/)
- Scope per guides: it applies to renewing services such as subscriptions or abonos. One guide notes the rule does not expressly exclude product-only sellers. The adaptation period ended in late 2025 — [tuwebfacil 2026](https://tuwebfacil.com.ar/blog/que-tiene-que-tener-tu-web-por-ley/)
- History: Res. 271/2020 required companies to publish contracts and the botón de baja on their main website — [argentina.gob.ar news](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/las-empresas-deben-publicar-los-contratos-y-el-boton-de-baja-en-su-web-principal); [Trade & Retail](https://tradeyretail.com/internet-obligan-a-incluir-contratos-y-boton-de-baja_1103). Res. 357/2021 extended the duty to more companies — [Noetinger & Armando](https://noetingeryarmando.com/se-amplia-a-mas-empresas-la-obligacion-del-boton-de-baja-resolucion-357-2021/)
- Statutory complement: art. 10 ter requires a free written cancellation receipt ("constancia fehaciente") within 72 hours, and cancellation by the same medium — [Juristeca CABA](https://juristeca.jusbaires.gob.ar/?p=2477)

### Inferences
- A Premium pass is a one-off payment that extends the pass by one month, with no automatic debit, so there is technically no recurring charge to stop. Even so, the client portal is an ongoing service relationship sold through the web. The safe reading of the scope ("comercialicen … servicios a distancia, a través de páginas web") is to **include the "BOTÓN DE BAJA DE SERVICIO"** on the home page.
- The baja flow should:
  - require no login;
  - send a code within 24 hours;
  - send the 72-hour constancia (art. 10 ter);
  - stop any future obligation;
  - state what happens to the remaining prepaid days (see section 9).

### Gaps
- It is not confirmed whether Disp. 954/2025 also expressly repealed Res. 271/2020 and Res. 357/2021, or kept their duty to publish contracts. The art. 38 statutory duty to publish the model contract stands in any case.
- No official guidance was found on whether one-off prepaid passes with no automatic renewal fall within the botón de baja scope.

## 5. E-commerce mandatory information: Res. 270/2020 (GMC 37/19), Res. 104/2005 (GMC 21/04), Res. 994/2018

### Takeaway
**Res. SCI 270/2020** incorporated **Mercosur GMC Res. 37/19** on consumer protection in e-commerce. It was published in the BO on 8-Sep-2020 and has been in force since 7-Mar-2021, with infractions sanctioned under Ley 24.240. I found no repeal. It requires the provider's identity and contact data, the essential terms, and the T&C **in an easy-to-see place before the contract is formalised**.

### Cited Findings
- Res. 270/2020 (Secretaría de Comercio Interior) incorporated GMC Res. 37/2019. BO 08-09-2020. Infractions are sanctioned under Ley 24.240. In force 180 days after publication, i.e. 7 March 2021 — [Erreius](https://www.erreius.com/Legislacion/documento/20200908073037576/resolucion-270-2020-incorporacion-al-ordenamiento-juridico-nacional-de-la-resolucion-37-2019-del-grupo-mercado-comun-del-mercosur-relativa-a-la-proteccion-al-consumidor-en-el-comercio-electronico); [Abeledo Gottheil](https://abeledogottheil.com.ar/en/consumer-protection-in-e-commerce/); [Microjuris](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2020/09/08/legislacion-comercio-electronico-se-incorpora-al-ordenamiento-juridico-nacional-una-resolucion-del-mercosur-sobre-proteccion-al-consumidor/); [Mercosur SIM normativa](https://normas.mercosur.int/public/normativas/3768)
- Information duty: the provider must make available "en su sitio web y demás medios electrónicos, en ubicación de fácil visualización y previo a la formalización del contrato" its nombre comercial y social, dirección física y electrónica, and the consumer-service e-mail, among other data — [LT10](https://www.lt10.com.ar/noticia/279421--nueva-normativa-para-el-comercio-electronico-); [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/proteccion-al-consumidor-en-el-comercio-electronico/27496)
- Rest of the GMC 37/19 list, from my knowledge of the Mercosur text (not verified in this session) — [Mercosur SIM](https://normas.mercosur.int/public/normativas/3768):
  - tax ID (CUIT);
  - essential characteristics of the service, including health or safety risks;
  - price including taxes, with any extra cost broken out;
  - payment methods, including instalments and costo financiero total;
  - terms, conditions and limits of the offer;
  - a contract summary highlighting the most significant clauses, before contracting;
  - a way to review and correct errors before confirming;

  - access to the contract in a form that can be stored and reproduced;
  - an efficient complaints channel;
  - clear information on the "derecho de arrepentimiento".

### Inferences
- The website footer or legal page should show:
  - razón social or name of the owner, plus the brand "Zar del Fitness";
  - CUIT;
  - the real physical address in Colegiales, not a PO box (art. 33 Ley 24.240);
  - e-mail and WhatsApp for consumer service.
- The T&C should be linked and accepted **before** payment in the portal, ideally with a short summary of key terms (price, duration, cancellation, revocation, refunds) shown before the checkout button.

The website URL is registered in the 960/D service with clave fiscal, and the logo is hyperlinked — [RG 4042-E/2017 texto original](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-274424/texto); [AFIP 960 normativa](https://www.afip.gob.ar/960/ayuda/normativa.asp); [argentina.gob.ar – Obtener el formulario de Data Fiscal](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/servicio/obtener-el-formulario-de-data-fiscal)

- Place the Data Fiscal QR or logo on the home page of zardelfitness.com.ar, typically in the footer, linked to ARCA. Register the domain in the F. 960/D service and regenerate it whenever the tax category changes, for example a monotributo category change.

## 7. Prices: display rules, IVA transparency (Ley 27.743 / Res. 4/2025), USD pricing, price increases for ongoing services

### Takeaway
**Res. SIC 4/2025** (BO 17-Jan-2025) **repealed Res. 7/2002 and Res. 55/2002**, the old price-display regime. It requires the **final price in pesos** (the total the consumer pays). It **allows an additional price in USD or another currency**, and services "desde, hacia y en el exterior" may be shown **only in USD**. From 1-Apr-2025 it also requires the net price without national indirect taxes, with the legend "**PRECIO SIN IMPUESTOS NACIONALES**". For price increases, case law fines gyms that raise fees without clear prior notice, and Disp. 377/2026 lists unilateral-modification clauses as abusive.

### Cited Findings
- Res. 4/2025 (Secretaría de Industria y Comercio), art. 6: "Deróganse las Resoluciones Nros. 7 de fecha 3 de junio de 2002 y sus modificatorias y 55 de fecha 20 de noviembre de 2002 y su modificatoria." Prices "además de ser exhibidos en pesos, podrán exhibirse en dólares estadounidenses u otra moneda extranjera". Prices of services "desde, hacia y en el exterior podrán ser exhibidos y publicitados exclusivamente en dólares estadounidenses". No specific exchange rate is mandated — [BO Res. 4/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/319787/20250117); [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/index.php/se-podra-exhibir-los-precios-en-pesos-argentinos-y-tambien-se-permite-la-exhibicion-adicional-en-dolares-estadounidenses-u-otra-moneda-extranjera/36204); [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/economia/el-gobierno-dispuso-que-los-comercios-podran-mostrar-los-precios-en-dolares-nid16012025/)
- The price shown must be the **total amount the consumer pays**. The rule applies to anyone offering goods or services directly to final consumers, including ads in any medium (art. 2 c) — [InfoLEG Res. 4/2025](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/405000-409999/408455/norma.htm); [argentina.gob.ar explainer PDF](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/exhibicion_de_precios_resolucion_4_2025.pdf)
- Net price: the amount without IVA and other national indirect taxes, "en caracteres menores" and with the legend "PRECIO SIN IMPUESTOS NACIONALES", **from 1-Apr-2025** — [argentina.gob.ar PDF](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/exhibicion_de_precios_resolucion_4_2025.pdf); [Canosa](https://canosa.com/es/nuevas-regulaciones-para-la-exhibicion-de-precios-al-consumidor/); [CAME](https://www.redcame.org.ar/novedades/14094/nuevas-disposiciones-sobre-la-exhibicion-de-precios-en-moneda-extranjera)
- E-commerce (art. 4): two media say that in online stores the net and final prices must use the **same size and type of font**. This conflicts with the "smaller characters" rule for physical shops — [DolarHoy](https://dolarhoy.com/economia/atencion-comercios-deberan-mostrar-precios-sin-impuestos-2025120115732); [Política Argentina](https://www.politicargentina.com/notas/202501/63538-los-comercios-deberan-exhibir-en-las-gondolas-los-precios-de-los-productos-sin-impuestos-a-partir-de-abril.html); [HOOD INT – online stores](https://www.int.com.ar/novedades/novedades/transparencia-fiscal-en-tiendas-catalogos-online-lo-que-exige-la-resolucion-4-2025/)
- Financing: if instalments are offered, the cash price, the instalments and the costo financiero total must be stated — [FECECO](https://fececo.org.ar/exhibicion-de-precios-inspecciones-para-verificar-el-cumplimiento-de-la-resolucion-4-2025-de-la-secretaria-de-industria-y-comercio-de-la-nacion/)
- Monotributistas: a billing blog says monotributistas issuing "C" invoices do not break out IVA, and that the invoice transparency change affects only Responsables Inscriptos. This is secondary and gives no rule citation — [evemuriel blog](https://evemuriel.com/blog/facturacion/transparencia-fiscal-facturas-con-iva-detallado.html)
- Gym price increases:
  - CNACAF (federal appeals court) confirmed an **ARS 50,000 fine on Gimnasios Argentinos S.A.** for not clearly reporting a fee increase in an automatic-debit dispute. The court rejected "consentimiento tácito" because the member kept using the service — [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/confirman-la-multa-impuesta-a-un-gimnasio-que-no-informo-el-incremento-de-las-cuotas-del-servicio-ofrecido/25970); [ruling PDF](https://abogados.com.ar/archivos/2020-05-25-105311-gimnasios-argentinos-sa-c-dndc-s-defensa-del-consumidor.pdf); [Estudio Rawson](https://estudiorawson.com.ar/la-importancia-del-deber-de-informacion-multan-a-un-gimnasio-por-omitir-informar-el-aumento-de-la-cuota/)
  - The Federal Chamber (Sala IV) confirmed a fine on a gym that debited ARS 385 at renewal when ARS 285 had been agreed — [Microjuris](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2016/09/20/se-multa-a-un-gimnasio-por-infraccion-a-la-ley-de-defensa-al-consumidor-al-haber-incurrido-en-incumplimiento-contractual-por-cobrar-una-cuota-superior-a-la-convenida/); [Comercio y Justicia](https://comercioyjusticia.info/justicia/confirman-procedencia-de-multa-de-la-ley-de-defensa-al-consumidor-contra-gimnasio-que-aumento-cuota/)
- Disp. 377/2026 reaffirms that clauses letting the provider modify or interpret the contract unilaterally are abusive — [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/defensa-del-consumidor-actualizacion-del-regimen-de-clausulas-abusivas-en-contratos-de-consumo/38725); [Noetinger & Armando](https://noetingeryarmando.com/actualizacion-del-regimen-de-clausulas-abusivas-para-contratos-de-consumo-en-argentina/)

### Inferences
- On the website, show each plan as a **final price in ARS including all taxes** (ARS 450,000 / 170,000 / 100,000). Add "Precio sin impuestos nacionales: ARS …" if the business is a Responsable Inscripto. For a monotributista the net-price treatment is unclear; see Gaps.
- The **international plan at USD 100** fits the "servicios desde, hacia y en el exterior" carve-out (USD only allowed) if it is sold to clients abroad. If it can be bought by Argentine residents, also show an ARS price.
- Price increases: the Premium pass is a prepaid one-off monthly payment, so the T&C should state that:
  - the price is the one shown at payment time;
  - already-paid periods are never re-priced;
  - changes apply only to future purchases, announced in advance by e-mail/WhatsApp and on the site (a reasonable notice such as 30 days is common practice but not a rule found here).
- For WhatsApp/in-gym plans, avoid any "we may change prices at any time" clause.

### Gaps
- **Ley 27.743 "Régimen de Transparencia Fiscal al Consumidor":** I could not retrieve its exact article numbers or confirm how it links to Res. 4/2025. From background knowledge only (unverified): Ley 27.743 (2024) requires showing IVA and other national indirect taxes on consumer invoices, implemented by an ARCA RG in late 2024/2025.
- I could not verify the official text of Res. 4/2025 art. 4 (online font-size rule) or whether monotributistas must show a "precio sin impuestos nacionales".
- I found no rule setting a minimum notice period for price increases on ongoing gym services.

## 8. Mandatory consumer-defence notices: Ventanilla Única Federal / COPREC (national) and CABA (Ley 757, Ley 2244, 147)

### Takeaway
- **National:** **Res. SCI 274/2021** requires providers with websites or apps to link to the **Ventanilla Única Federal (VUF)** complaint form, with the text "Defensa de las y los Consumidores. Para reclamos ingrese aquí". COPREC was **dissolved by Decreto 55/2025** from 1-Feb-2025, and the VUF is now the central national channel.
- **CABA:** CABA adhered to the VUF by **Disp. 423/DGDYPC/2025**. **CABA Ley 2244** requires businesses serving CABA consumers that have a website to include a link to the Dirección General de Defensa y Protección al Consumidor (line 147). The procedure is Ley 757.

### Cited Findings
- Res. 274/2021 (Secretaría de Comercio Interior) created the VUF and provides that "los proveedores que posean páginas o aplicaciones web deberán incorporar un enlace que permita el ingreso a la página correspondiente al formulario de denuncia de la Ventanilla Única Federal". Link text: "Defensa de las y los Consumidores / Para reclamos / Ingrese aquí". Deadline: 60 days, from about 29 May 2021 — [abogados.com.ar – obligación de enlace](https://abogados.com.ar/defensa-del-consumidor-obligacion-de-establecer-un-enlace-link-a-la-ventanilla-unica-federal/28038); [abogados.com.ar – VUF](https://abogados.com.ar/ventanilla-unica-federal-en-el-ambito-de-la-direccion-nacional-de-defensa-al-consumidor/28028); [Erreius](https://www.erreius.com/actualidad/10/comercial-empresarial-y-del-consumidor/Nota/1120/defensa-del-consumidor-se-crea-la-ventanilla-unica-federal-para-facilitar-reclamos-de-los-consumidores)
- Decreto 55/2025 dissolved COPREC, its conciliator registry and its fund, **in force from 1-Feb-2025**. Pending cases moved to the Secretaría de Industria y Comercio, and the VUF distributes complaints nationwide — [Decreto 55/2025 texto](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-409013/texto&gsessionid=bRn822YUGUqNYJMQEnmX8vDZRPfNTkwC3kblZ5M7VZ3FA7yg3HKfWw); [Beccar Varela](https://beccarvarela.com/en/novedades/decreto-55-2025-disoluciones/); [0221](https://www.0221.com.ar/nacional/el-gobierno-disuelve-el-coprec-y-transfiere-los-reclamos-consumo-la-ventanilla-unica-federal-n104694)

## 9. Subscription / membership practices (freezing, transfers, refunds, cancellation via WhatsApp, advance payment) and gym sanctions

### Takeaway
Argentine authorities and courts have sanctioned gyms mainly for:
1. charging more than agreed on automatic-debit renewals;
2. not clearly reporting fee increases;
3. obstructing cancellation (arts. 10 ter / 10 quater).

No specific national gym regulation was found. Freezing and transfer policies are contractual but must not be abusive.

- Gimnasios Argentinos S.A. c/ DNDC (CNACAF, 2020): ARS 50,000 fine for not clearly reporting a fee increase in an automatic-debit context; tacit consent was rejected — [ruling PDF](https://abogados.com.ar/archivos/2020-05-25-105311-gimnasios-argentinos-sa-c-dndc-s-defensa-del-consumidor.pdf); [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/confirman-la-multa-impuesta-a-un-gimnasio-que-no-informo-el-incremento-de-las-cuotas-del-servicio-ofrecido/25970)

  - Sources: [Infobae via search extract](https://www.infobae.com/politica/2026/03/11/ley-de-defensa-al-consumidor-el-ministerio-de-economia-actualizo-el-listado-de-clausulas-abusivas-en-contratos/); [abogados.com.ar](https://abogados.com.ar/defensa-del-consumidor-actualizacion-del-regimen-de-clausulas-abusivas-en-contratos-de-consumo/38725); [El Litoral](https://www.ellitoral.com/nacionales/clausulas-abusivas-contratos-consumidor-defensaconsumidor-proteccion-contratos-argentina-serviciosdigitales-suscripciones-tutela-consumidor_0_MTsS6u9FkQ.html); [mariovadillo – 21 cláusulas](https://mariovadillo.com.ar/21-clausulas-abusivas-que-tu-contrato-no-puede-tener-aunque-las-hayas-firmado/)
- Disp. 377/2026 is said to apply to existing and future contracts, which may be disputed. Sources disagree on the number of items (21 vs. "seven new") — [Canal 26 via search](https://www.canal26.com/economia/2026/03/11/ley-de-defensa-al-consumidor-los-detalles-de-la-ampliacion-de-la-normativa-para-evitar-abusos-en-los-contratos/); [RCTZZ](https://rctzz.com.ar/es/insights/%E2%9A%A0%EF%B8%8F-legal-alert-defensa-del-consumidor-nuevas-clausulas-abusivas-en-contratos-de-consumo)

### Inferences
- **Cancellation via WhatsApp:** must be accepted for WhatsApp-contracted plans (art. 10 ter). Reply with a written constancia within 72 hours and with no charge (art. 10 quater).
- **Advance payment:** prepaying the month is lawful. Already-paid periods cannot be re-priced (art. 19). Within 10 days of a distance contract the consumer may revoke for a refund (CCyC 1110).
- **Refunds of unused prepaid time after day 10:** no rule found mandating prorated refunds for a voluntary mid-month cancellation of a one-month prepaid pass. A clause that the current month is not refunded but nothing further is owed is the common market practice; it is consistent with art. 10 quater, which bars only *extra* charges. If the gym cannot provide the service (closure, illness of the coach, force majeure), Disp. 377/2026 makes clauses shifting caso fortuito risk to the consumer abusive, so offer credit or a refund.
- **Freezing/transfers:** permissible as benefits. Define the conditions clearly (art. 4) and do not reserve unilateral changes (Disp. 377/2026).
- **Bans:** do not include:
  - penalties for negative reviews;
  - "acceptance by browsing";
  - unilateral termination without cause by the gym;
  - mandatory annual lock-ins with exit fees (conflicts with art. 10 quater);
  - automatic renewals not clearly agreed.
- The Premium model, with no automatic debit, avoids the most-sanctioned gym practice.

### Gaps
- I found no 2024–2026 news of specific CABA or national sanctions against gyms; the searches only returned 2016–2020 rulings.
- No official rule was found on membership freezing or transfers.
- I could not verify the full numbered list of Disp. 377/2026, including whether it expressly addresses automatic renewal or cancellation procedures.

## 10. Waivers / limitation of liability toward consumers (Ley 24.240 art. 37; CCyC 1743) — what can and cannot be written

### Takeaway
Any clause that **limits or excludes liability for damages** toward a consumer is treated as **not agreed** (Ley 24.240 art. 37 a). It is also invalid under CCyC art. 1743 when it affects non-waivable rights, is abusive, or covers dolo. Providers must offer services that pose no danger in normal, foreseeable use (Ley 24.240 art. 5). What a gym can lawfully write is **information and allocation of the client's own duties**, such as a health declaration, following instructor guidance and reporting conditions. It cannot write a waiver.

### Cited Findings
- Ley 24.240 art. 37 a): "las cláusulas que desnaturalicen las obligaciones o limiten la responsabilidad por daños" are not agreed. Art. 37 b) covers clauses that waive or restrict consumer rights; art. 37 c) covers reversal of the burden of proof. Official consolidated text (not re-fetched) — [argentina.gob.ar Ley 24.240](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/638/actualizacion)
- CCyC art. 1743 ("Dispensa anticipada de la responsabilidad"), official text (not re-fetched): "Son inválidas las cláusulas que eximen o limitan la obligación de indemnizar cuando afectan derechos indisponibles, atentan contra la buena fe, las buenas costumbres o leyes imperativas, o son abusivas. Son también inválidas si liberan anticipadamente, en forma total o parcial, del daño sufrido por dolo del deudor o de las personas por las cuales debe responder." — [InfoLEG CCyC](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/235000-239999/235975/norma.htm)
- Ley 24.240 art. 5, official text (not re-fetched): "Las cosas y servicios deben ser suministrados o prestados en forma tal que, utilizados en condiciones previsibles o normales de uso, no presenten peligro alguno para la salud o integridad física de los consumidores o usuarios." Art. 6 requires instructions for risky services. Art. 40 sets joint and strict liability for damage from "la prestación del servicio" — [argentina.gob.ar Ley 24.240](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/638/actualizacion)
- Disp. 377/2026 lists as abusive any clause shifting caso fortuito or fuerza mayor consequences to the consumer — [Allende & Brea](https://allende.com/privacidad-y-ciberseguridad/nuevo-listado-de-clausulas-abusivas-en-contratos-de-consumo-03-18-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Cannot write:**
  - "Zar del Fitness no se responsabiliza por lesiones…";
  - "el cliente renuncia a reclamar";
  - caps on damages;
  - "el cliente asume todos los riesgos";
  - exclusion of liability for staff;
  - reversal of the burden of proof.
- **Can write:**
  - a duty to declare health conditions and present a medical fitness certificate where required;
  - a duty to follow the coach's indications and the gym rules;
  - an explanation of the inherent risks of training (information, not exemption);
  - that online plans are performed by the client in their own environment, with guidance on safe use;
  - that liability is governed by Ley 24.240 and the CCyC.
- Lost-items clauses ("no nos responsabilizamos por objetos personales") are risky under art. 37 a). Frame them as a recommendation not to leave valuables, plus a description of the lockers provided.

### Gaps
- The CABA gym-specific rules (medical fitness certificate, registry; possibly CABA Ley 139) were outside this scope and not researched.

## 11. Mercado Pago seller obligations relevant to T&C (refunds, chargebacks)

### Takeaway
In Mercado Pago Checkout Pro, the disputed amount is held during a chargeback. If the chargeback is upheld, the funds are withdrawn from the seller and returned to the buyer. Mercado Pago recommends cancelling and refunding when it raises a fraud alert. I found no 180-day figure and could not retrieve MP's seller terms.

### Cited Findings
- Checkout Pro chargebacks (Argentina docs):
  - the disputed amount stays held in the seller's account until the dispute is resolved;
  - if the chargeback is valid, the charge is annulled and the funds go back to the buyer;
  - sellers should turn on chargeback notifications and send documentation through the API when asked.
  - Source: [Mercado Pago Developers AR – Checkout Pro chargebacks](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/es/docs/checkout-pro/chargebacks); [MP Developers AR – Checkout Pro preferences chargebacks](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/es/docs/checkout-pro-preferences/chargebacks/introduction?scope=prod)
- Mercado Pago recommends that a seller who receives a fraud alert cancel the purchase and return the money to avoid a chargeback. Non-delivery or late delivery is a common dispute reason — [MP Developers MX](https://www.mercadopago.com.mx/developers/es/docs/checkout-pro/chargebacks); [MP Developers CL](https://www.mercadopago.cl/developers/es/docs/checkout-pro/chargebacks)

### Inferences
- The T&C should say:
  - payments are processed by Mercado Pago, subject to its terms;
  - refunds for revocation (button) or service failure go back **through the same payment method** via Mercado Pago, at no cost to the consumer: no deduction of MP fees (CCyC 1115; art. 34 "sin responsabilidad alguna");
  - the timing of the credit depends on the card issuer or MP.
- Good practice is to keep payment-to-pass records (portal logs, ID of the extended pass) as evidence in chargeback disputes.
- No automatic debit is used. The T&C should say so explicitly, because it reassures clients and avoids the debit-related sanctions above.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve Mercado Pago's "Términos y condiciones de uso" (seller section), its refund deadline (the commonly cited 180-day refund window is unverified), or any MP rule requiring merchants to publish a refund policy.
